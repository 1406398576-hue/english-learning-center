Talking About Work

聊聊工作 · 忙、跳槽、还是"先干着吧"
"It's not just a job. It's a conversation starter."
💬 核心表达
I'm swamped. 我忙成狗了 / 被工作淹没了
回答别人"最近怎么样"时，比 I'm very busy 更地道。swamp 原意是沼泽，这里作动词表示「被淹没」。
影视原句
"I'm swamped. I haven't even had lunch."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S03E19 / 加班赶方案时的经典抱怨
"I'm swamped here, Michael. Can this wait?"
— Jim Halpert, The Office S02E06 / 手头一堆文件时的敷衍回复
"I'm totally swamped at work right now."
— Marshall Eriksen, How I Met Your Mother S03E07 / 解释自己为什么没空赴约
I'm thinking about moving on. 我在考虑换工作 / 想撤了
比直接说 I want to quit my job 更含蓄、更成熟。move on 表示「往前走/换条路」，适合跟朋友或同事轻描淡写地提。
影视原句
"I'm thinking about quitting my job."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S01E15 / Chandler 对 data reconfiguration 忍无可忍
"I think it might be time for me to move on."
— Jim Halpert, The Office S03E18 / 谈到是否留在 Dunder Mifflin 时
"I quit my job today. I'm ready to move on."
— Marshall Eriksen, How I Met Your Mother S04E08 / 辞掉律所工作后的宣言
It pays the bills. 能糊口 / 反正够付账单
别人问你对工作的感受，你不想吐槽也不想夸，就用这句轻描淡写。语气带点「不热爱但实用」的味道。
影视原句
"It's not my dream job, but it pays the bills."
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S06E20 / 谈到商业广告和临时演出时
"I sell paper. It pays the bills."
— Stanley Hudson, The Office S04E04 / Stanley 对自己工作的极简总结
"Suit up. It pays the bills, baby."
— Barney Stinson, How I Met Your Mother S02E07 / Barney 对自己西装人生的辩护
I have back-to-back meetings. 我会议一个接一个 / 连轴开会
back-to-back 是「背靠背」，用来形容会议、课程排得满满当当。是职场里最常用的抱怨句式之一。
影视原句
"I have back-to-back meetings all afternoon."
— Monica Geller, Friends S08E16 / 解释自己没法早退
"I've been in meetings all day. I don't even know what day it is."
— Michael Scott, The Office S05E09 / 典型的 Michael 式夸张吐槽
"I'm stuck in meetings all day. Dinner's on you."
— Claire Dunphy, Modern Family S03E15 / 职场妈妈回家前给家人的留言
I'm burning out. 我要累垮了 / 快 burnout 了
burn out 不是单纯 tired，而是长期高压下的「燃尽」。用来描述工作强度已经影响身心，比 I'm tired 重得多。
影视原句
"I'm burning out here. I need a break."
— Rachel Green, Friends S08E12 / 工作带娃两头烧时的崩溃边缘
"I'm burning out. I'm making mistakes I never make."
— Pam Beesly, The Office S05E15 / 承认自己被工作压垮
"I'm burning out and I don't know how to say no."
— Lily Aldrin, How I Met Your Mother S01E09 / 幼儿园老师+Lily 的双重身份压力
🎭 情景对话
场景：周四午饭时间，Alex 和 Jordan 在公司附近的三明治店排队。两人都在同一家公司不同部门。
Jordan
Hey, you look like you haven't slept.
嘿，你看着像没睡觉一样。
Alex
Tell me about it. I'm swamped. I have back-to-back meetings until four.
可不是嘛。我忙成狗了，下午四点前会议一个接一个。
Jordan
Same here. My manager just dumped another project on me.
我也是。我经理刚又扔了一个项目给我。
Alex
Honestly? I'm burning out. I've been thinking about moving on.
说实话吗？我要累垮了。我在考虑换工作。
Jordan
Really? I had no idea. What's making you want to leave?
真的？我完全不知道。是什么让你想走？
Alex
It's not terrible — it pays the bills. But there's no room to grow. I'm just exhausted.
也没那么糟，能糊口。但完全没有成长空间。我就是 exhausted 了。
Jordan
I get it. I felt like that last year. Have you started looking yet?
我懂。我去年也是这感觉。你开始找了吗？
Alex
Just casually updated my LinkedIn. Nothing serious yet. Let's talk later — I need to grab this sandwich and run.
就随便更新了 LinkedIn，还没认真找。晚点再聊——我得拿了三明治赶紧跑。
🎤 口语练习
1 A friend asks, "How's work going?" Answer using "I'm swamped" or "back-to-back meetings."
你可以这样说
A："Honestly? I'm swamped. I don't even remember what I had for breakfast."
B："It's been crazy. I have back-to-back meetings today, so I barely have time to eat."
C："Work's work, you know? Busy, but not terrible. I'm just swamped this week."
2 Tell a close friend you're thinking about leaving your job. Give one reason.
你可以这样说
A："I've been thinking about moving on. There's no room to grow, and I'm kind of bored."
B："I'm considering quitting. The workload is just too much, and I'm burning out."
C："I think it might be time for a change. I've been there three years and it feels like the same day over and over."
3 Describe a job you don't love but keeps you going. Use "It pays the bills."
你可以这样说
A："It's not my dream job, but it pays the bills. And my coworkers are nice, so that helps."
B："I do admin work. It's pretty boring, but it pays the bills and I can leave at five."
C："Honestly? I just push papers all day. It pays the bills while I figure out what I actually want to do."
⚠️ 纠正提醒
"My work is very busy."
"I'm swamped." / "Work has been crazy." / "It's been a busy week."
中文常说「我的工作很忙」，但英语里更自然的主语是人，用 I'm swamped / I'm slammed / I'm buried in work。说 work is busy 语法没错，但更像「这份工作本身忙」，不自然。
"I want to change job."
"I'm thinking about switching jobs." / "I'm looking for a new job."
change job 听起来像「换工作内容」，而不是「换一家公司」。英语里「换工作」常用 switch jobs / change jobs；更自然也可以说 I'm thinking about moving on / I'm ready for something new。
"My salary is not high."
"It doesn't pay much." / "The pay isn't great." / "I'm not making much."
直接说 salary is not high 太中式。英语里用 pay 作名词更口语：The pay is low / It doesn't pay well / I'm not getting paid enough。也可以幽默地说 It pays the bills — 暗示刚好够。
"I have many meetings today."
"I'm in meetings all day." / "I have back-to-back meetings."
many meetings 太像课本英语。all day / back-to-back 更生动地形容「排满」。back-to-back 尤其强调一个接一个，中间没休息，是职场高频表达。
"I am very tired because of work."
"I'm burned out." / "I'm worn out." / "Work's been draining."
tired because of work 太直译。burned out 表示长期工作高压下的「燃尽感」，worn out 更偏向「累坏了」，draining 表示「消耗精力」。选哪个取决于你想表达的疲惫程度。
