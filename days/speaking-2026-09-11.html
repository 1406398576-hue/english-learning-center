B · Short Story
The Notebook

I was cleaning out a drawer when I found it — a small notebook I hadn't opened in maybe six years. Most pages were grocery lists and half-finished to-do lists. But on page 34, in my handwriting from a different era, there was a phone number with one word next to it: "Dad."

My parents split up when I was nine. I saw my father on weekends, then once a month, then less. By the time I was seventeen, we'd stopped calling each other entirely. It wasn't a fight. It was just — silence that thickened until neither of us could cut through it.

I stared at the number for a long time. My thumb hovered over the screen. I could call. I could hear his voice. He'd probably pick up. He'd probably say my name in that way that used to make me feel like the only person in the room.

But I didn't. I closed the notebook, put it back in the drawer, and went to make coffee. Not because I don't care. Because I'm afraid that if I call and he's changed — or worse, if he hasn't — I'll have to feel everything I've been keeping quiet for fifteen years.

C · Key Expressions
5 Expressions Worth Learning
cut through (something)
穿透、打破（沉默/隔阂/障碍）
"She tried to cut through the awkwardness with a joke, but nobody laughed."
常用于抽象语境：cut through the noise / the silence / the tension。比 break 更有"穿透一层厚东西"的画面感。
hover over
悬停在……上方（犹豫不决）
"My cursor hovered over the send button for two full minutes."
物理意义是"悬浮在上方"，日常口语常用来描述犹豫时刻——手指悬着、鼠标悬着、目光悬着。暗示"差一点就做了但没做"。
thicken
变浓、变厚（可形容沉默、气氛、雾等）
"The silence between them thickened until someone had to leave the room."
silent/thick 的进阶用法。说 silence thickened 比 saying it was awkward 更有身体感——像液体慢慢凝固。也用于 fog / plot。
in a different era
在完全不同的时期/时代（比喻"过去的自己"）
"I found old journal entries from 2015 — it feels like they were written in a different era."
不一定是历史时代。日常用来感叹"那时候的我跟现在的我差太多"。比 saying "a long time ago" 多了一层"人变了"的唏嘘。
keep (something) quiet
把某事压在心里不说、不表露
"He'd kept his doubts about the marriage quiet for three years before finally telling her."
比 hide 更温和——不是刻意隐瞒，而是"一直没提"。常用于情感、意见、不满。口语比 suppress / conceal 自然得多。
