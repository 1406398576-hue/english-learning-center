At the Gym & Working Out

健身房与运动健身 · 从热身到收工的真实表达
"I'm just trying to get back into it, you know? Baby steps."
💬 核心表达
I'm trying to get back into shape. 我想恢复锻炼/恢复身材
好久不运动，重新开始健身时的标准开场白。get back into shape = 恢复（原来的）体型/状态。shape 在口语里常指身体状况，in good shape = 身体状态好。比说 I want to do exercise 自然一百倍，也不显得你在找借口。
影视原句
"I haven't been to the gym in like six months. I'm just trying to get back into shape before my sister's wedding — don't want to look like a potato in the photos."
— Monica Geller, Friends S06E02 / 向 Rachel 解释为什么突然开始疯狂健身
"New Year's resolution — I'm trying to get back into shape. So far I've been to the gym twice and eaten four cheeseburgers. The math isn't working."
— Andy Bernard, The Office S04E10 / 新年决心后的自嘲
"The doctor said my cholesterol is through the roof, so I'm trying to get back into shape. I bought a treadmill. It's currently being used as a coat rack."
— Jay Pritchett, Modern Family S05E08 / 向 Gloria 半开玩笑地解释健身动机
Could you give me a spot? 能帮我保护一下吗？
在健身房做卧推、深蹲等需要保护的重量训练时，请人帮忙看着。spot = 在旁边保护，防止你举不起来时被砸到。give me a spot 是最简洁自然的说法，也可以说 Could you spot me? 注意千万别说 protect me——那是"保护我免受伤害"的意思，不是健身搭话的用语。
影视原句
"Hey, could you give me a spot? I'm going for a personal best today — 225. If I die, tell my mother I loved her."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S05E04 / 在健身房请 Ross 帮忙保护卧推
"Dwight, could you give me a spot? And by 'spot' I mean stand there silently and don't make it weird."
— Jim Halpert, The Office S03E20 / 请 Dwight 帮忙时的经典 sarcasm
"Excuse me, could you spot me real quick? I just need someone to hand me the weights — my arms are basically noodles right now."
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S07E15 / 练到力竭后礼貌求助陌生人
I'm feeling the burn. 我感受到燃烧感了（练到位了）
锻炼中肌肉发酸、发热，说明练到位了。the burn = 那种肌肉在"燃烧"的酸胀感。这个词在健身文化里几乎是通用语，既可以认真说"我练到了"，也可以半开玩笑地喊"要死了"。Jane Fonda 当年靠这词火遍健身界，到现在美国人还天天说。
影视原句
"Oh, I'm feeling the burn. And by 'the burn' I mean my entire body is on fire and I regret every life choice that led me here."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S04E12 / 在 Ross 监督下做俯卧撑时的吐槽
"Pam, you feeling the burn? Good. Embrace the burn. The burn is your friend. The burn means you're alive."
— Michael Scott, The Office S02E11 / 带着员工做办公室"健身"时的激情喊话
"I'm feeling the burn, Dad! Is this what you feel every day? No wonder you're so grumpy before coffee."
— Alex Dunphy, Modern Family S06E10 / 第一次跟 Jay 晨跑时的喘气吐槽
I think I overdid it. 我觉得练过头了
练得太猛，第二天浑身酸痛走不动路时的经典感慨。overdo = 过度/做过头，overdid 是过去式。it 指代今天的锻炼。也可以说 I went too hard today 或 I pushed myself too hard。健身新手最容易犯的错就是第一天太兴奋练过量，这句话你大概率用得上。
影视原句
"I think I overdid it at the gym. I can't lift my arms. I had to brush my teeth with my elbow this morning."
— Ross Geller, Friends S03E08 / 向 Monica 描述练过头后的惨状
"I went for a five-mile run on my first day. I think I overdid it. My legs are sending me hate mail."
— Pam Beesly, The Office S05E06 / 对着镜头展示走路一瘸一拐
"Dad made me do his 'light workout.' I think I overdid it. I can't feel my legs. Is this what being old feels like?"
— Haley Dunphy, Modern Family S04E18 / 跟 Jay 练完后瘫在沙发上
Let's call it a day. 今天就到这儿吧
练得差不多了，决定结束今天的训练。call it a day = 到此为止、收工。原本是工作场景用语（下班了），但在健身房也很常用——毕竟锻炼也是一种"work"。比说 I want to stop 或 I don't want to do it anymore 积极得多，带着"今天已经够努力了"的满足感。

— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S06E14 / 练完后跟 Chandler 提议收工

— Jim Halpert, The Office S04E15 / 跟 Dwight 结束午间锻炼
"Okay, I did ten minutes. That's basically a marathon for me. Let's call it a day and get smoothies."
— Cameron Tucker, Modern Family S03E22 / 以奖励自己的方式结束极短锻炼
🎭 情景对话
场景：周三傍晚，Alex 在健身房器械区犹豫地看着哑铃架。Jordan 是这里的常客，刚做完一组训练，注意到 Alex 有点不知所措，主动搭话。
Jordan
Hey, you look a little lost. First time here?
嘿，你看起来有点茫然。第一次来？
Alex
Kind of. I used to work out all the time, but I stopped for almost a year. I'm just trying to get back into shape.
算是吧。我以前经常锻炼，但停了快一年了。就是想恢复一下身材。
Jordan
I feel you. I did the same thing after the pandemic. Took me three months just to get back into a routine. Don't go too hard on day one.
我懂。疫情后我也这样。花了三个月才恢复规律。第一天别太猛。
Alex
Yeah, that's what I'm afraid of. I'm actually about to do some bench press — could you give me a spot?
是啊，我就是怕这个。我正要做卧推——能帮我保护一下吗？
Jordan
Sure, no problem. What are you aiming for?
没问题。你打算推多少？
Alex
Just 135 for now. I used to do 185, but... baby steps, right?
先推135吧。我以前能推185，但……循序渐进嘛，对吧？
Jordan
Smart. Alright, I'll be right here. Go for it.
明智。好，我就在旁边。来吧。
Alex
*pushes through the set* Okay, wow. I'm feeling the burn already. That was only eight reps and my arms are shaking.
*做完一组* 好吧，哇。已经有燃烧感了。才做了八个，手臂就在抖。
Jordan
That's a good sign. But seriously — don't do more today. I think you overdid it your first week, and you won't come back for a month.
这是好现象。但说真的——今天别再做了。我怕你第一周就练过头，然后一个月都不来了。
Alex
You're probably right. Let's call it a day. Thanks for the spot — I owe you one.
你说得对。今天就到这儿吧。谢谢帮忙保护——欠你一次。
🎤 口语练习
1 A coworker asks, "Do you work out?" Tell them about your current fitness routine — or lack of one — using "I'm trying to get back into shape."
你可以这样说
A："I do, kind of. I was really consistent last year, but I fell off the wagon. Now I'm trying to get back into shape — I started going to the gym twice a week, just trying to build the habit again."
B："Not really, to be honest. I keep saying I'm trying to get back into shape, but then I find excuses. This month I finally bought a gym membership, so we'll see how long that lasts."
C："Yeah, I do yoga and some light cardio. Nothing crazy — I'm not trying to get back into shape for a marathon or anything. I just want to feel less tired by 3 PM."
2 You're at the gym and want to ask a stranger to spot you on the bench press. How do you approach them politely?
你可以这样说
A："Excuse me, would you mind giving me a spot? Just for one set — I won't take much of your time. I'll only need help if I can't get the bar back up."
B："Hey, sorry to bother you. Could you give me a quick spot? I'm going pretty light today, so it should be easy — just need someone there in case."
C："Hi, could you spot me real quick? I'll return the favor if you need one later. Just stand behind me and grab the bar if my arms give out."
3 It's the day after an intense workout, and your friend asks why you're walking like a robot. Explain that you overdid it at the gym.
你可以这样说
A："Oh man, I think I overdid it at the gym yesterday. My trainer made me do squats until my legs turned to jelly. Today I can't even sit down without making a noise."
B："I got way too motivated and did leg day for the first time in months. I think I overdid it — my quads are so sore that walking down stairs is basically a horror movie."
C："I tried to keep up with this guy who was benching twice my weight. Bad idea. I think I overdid it, and now my everything hurts. Let's just say I'm not going back tomorrow."
⚠️ 纠正提醒
❌ I do exercise every day. / I make exercise.
✅ I work out every day. / I exercise every day.
do exercise 不是地道说法，make exercise 更是中式直译。日常说"锻炼"用 work out（动词短语）或 exercise（动词）。I work out 是最自然的表达，美国人说健身几乎都用这个词。
❌ I want to lose my weight.
✅ I'm trying to lose weight. / I'm trying to get in shape.
lose weight 中间不加 my——weight 在这里是抽象概念，不是"你的体重数字"。另外 I'm trying to get in shape 比直说"减肥"更积极自然，老外健身很少说 lose weight，更多是 get in shape / get fit / stay healthy。
❌ My muscle is painful. / My body is painful.
✅ I'm sore. / My muscles are sore. / I'm aching all over.
painful = 造成疼痛的（如 a painful wound），不是"感到疼痛"。人感到痛要说 sore / aching。健身后的肌肉酸痛最地道的说法就是 I'm sore 或 My [body part] is sore。也可以说 I have sore muscles。
❌ I go to gym. / I go to the GYM.
✅ I go to the gym. / I hit the gym.
gym 是可数名词，前面要加 the——I go to the gym。口语里更酷的说法是 I hit the gym，hit = 去（某地），类似 hit the road（出发）。这是健身房场景最高频的表达之一。
❌ Can you protect me? / Please help me lift.
✅ Could you give me a spot? / Could you spot me?
protect me 是"保护我免受攻击"的意思，健身时完全不对。请人帮忙"保护"（防止杠铃砸下来）要用 spot / give me a spot。help me lift 听起来像你要偷懒让人帮你举，也不对。spot 是健身专属动词，记住它。
