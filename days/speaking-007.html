今日主题

社交·初次见面破冰

Breaking the Ice with Strangers

派对、聚会、或是陌生场合，怎么自然地开口、大方介绍自己、把两个不认识的人牵线，再接上一句寒暄？今天这 5 个表达 + 1 段对话，练完就能直接用。

核心表达 · 5个高频说法（每条 3 个美剧真实台词）

1. "How you doin'?"
"你还好吗？／最近怎么样？"（Joey 的招牌开场白，用来和陌生人轻松搭话）
使用场景想跟刚认识的人打开话题，尤其是带点俏皮、轻松的破冰招呼
"Hey. How you doin'?" 《Friends》S04E13 "The One with Rachel's Crush" — Joey 教 Chandler 搭讪时示范这句
"How you doin'?" 《Friends》S07E13 "The One With Joey's New Brain" — Joey 在派对上对陌生女性使用招牌台词
"How you doin'?" 《Friends》经典口头禅（Joey Tribbiani 多集使用，如 S07E13 等）
2. "Have you met [name]?"
"你见过 [某人] 吗？"（Barney 用它来把两个陌生人牵线介绍）
使用场景你认识双方但不认识彼此时，主动帮他们破冰、互相介绍
"Hi, have you met Ted?" 《How I Met Your Mother》S01E01 "Pilot" — Barney 拍拍 Yasmin 肩膀，把 Ted 介绍给她
"Ted really liked her so we played 'Have you met Ted?'" 《How I Met Your Mother》S08E01 "Farhampton" — Barney 回忆当年就是用这招让 Ted 认识 Robin
"There's a girl that you have to meet. She is perfect for you. Have You Met Ted." 《How I Met Your Mother》S09E24 "Last Forever" — Barney 又要帮 Ted 牵线时说出经典台词
3. "Hi, I'm [name]..."（大方又别太啰嗦地介绍自己）
"嗨，我是 [某某]……"（下面都是 Michael 的"反面教材"——话太多反而更紧张）
使用场景刚进一个圈子，向一群陌生人做自我介绍；记住：简短就好
"Hi I'm Michael Scott, I'm in charge of Dunder Mifflin paper products here in Scranton, Pennsylvania." 《The Office》S01E02 "Diversity Day" — Michael 在多元文化训练上长篇大论地自我介绍
"Hi, I'm Michael Scott. And, for the next 40 minutes, I am going to be your tour guide through the lives of Phyllis Lapin and Bob Vance..." 《The Office》S03E04 "Phyllis' Wedding" — 婚礼致辞开场，又把"我是谁"说了两遍
"Hello. I'm Michael Scott. I'm the best boss in the world." 《The Office》S01E01 "Pilot"（删减片段）— Michael 对着镜头夸张地介绍自己
4. "My name is [name]." / "I'm [name]."（最简版的交换名字）
"我叫 [某某]。"（认识新朋友时，干净利落先报上名字）
使用场景握手或刚被介绍时，自然说出自己的名字，开启对话
"My name is Jim Halpert and I am a sales rep, which is a very important job." 《The Office》S01E01 "Pilot" — Jim 对着纪录片镜头大方介绍自己
"Hi, I'm Ted." — "Yasmin." — "It's a very pretty name." — "Thanks. It's Lebanese." 《How I Met Your Mother》S01E01 "Pilot" — Barney 一句 "have you met Ted" 后，Ted 和 Yasmin 交换名字
"I'm the cool dad. That's my thang. I'm hip, I surf the web, I text." 《Modern Family》S01E01 "Pilot" — Phil 一出场就用招牌方式介绍自己的"人设"
5. "How's it going?" / "How are you doing?"
"最近怎么样？／还顺利吗？"（报完名字后，最自然的寒暄开场白）
使用场景打完招呼、交换名字之后，用一句轻松的 small talk 让对话延续
"Hey gorgeous, how's it going?" 《Friends》S01E08 "The One Where Nana Dies Twice" — Shelley 在 Central Perk 跟 Chandler 打招呼
"How are you doing?" — "I'm okay." — "Ooh, that bad, huh?" 《Friends》S02E01 "The One with Ross's New Girlfriend" — Joey 用这句关心 Rachel 的状态
"Hey! How's it going guys?" 《Friends》S05E10 "The One with the Inappropriate Sister" — Joey 扮演"帅哥"出场时的开场白

情景对话 · 8轮真实聊天

🎉 场景：朋友 Lily 的 housewarming party（暖房派对）。Alex 和 Mia 互不相识，Lily 主动帮他们破冰。
Lily: Hey, you two don't know each other yet, right? Mia, this is Alex — he's a friend from my old job. Alex, this is Mia, my neighbor.
嘿，你们俩还不认识吧？Mia，这是 Alex，我以前同事的朋友。Alex，这是我邻居 Mia。
Alex: Hi, I'm Alex. Nice to meet you.
嗨，我是 Alex。很高兴认识你。
Mia: I'm Mia. Likewise! So, how do you know Lily?
我是 Mia。我也是！那你怎么认识 Lily 的？
Alex: We used to work together a few years ago. How about you — are you from around here?
我们几年前一起工作过。你呢——你是这一带的人吗？
Mia: Just moved in down the hall last month. So, Alex, what do you do?
上个月才搬来同层。对了 Alex，你是做什么工作的？
Alex: I'm a graphic designer. And you?
我是平面设计师。你呢？
Mia: I'm in marketing. Anyway, how's it going? Are you enjoying the party?
我做市场营销。话说回来，还顺利吗？派对玩得开心吗？
Alex: Yeah, it's great. Hey — have you met Tom over there? He's also into design. Come say hi!
嗯，特别棒。对了——你见过那边那个 Tom 吗？他也搞设计。过去打个招呼吧！

口语练习 · 大声说出来

  1. You're at a party and see someone standing alone. How would you walk over and start a conversation?
    💡 用上今天的 "Hi, I'm [name]." 再加一句破冰，比如 "How's it going?" 或 "How do you know the host?"
    示例： "Hi, I'm Alex. I don't think we've met. How do you know Lily?" "Hey, I'm Mia. How's it going? This is a great party, right?" "Hi, I'm Sam. Are you friends with the host, or just here for the snacks like me?"
  2. A friend introduces you to a stranger. What are three things you could say to keep the conversation going?
    💡 试试这些方向：问对方怎么认识主人、问职业（"What do you do?"）、或者接一句 "How's it going?"
    示例： "Nice to meet you. So, how do you know Chris?" "Great to meet you. What do you do for a living?" "Hi, I'm Alex. How's your night going so far?"
  3. Think of a recent event you went to. In English, describe how you met one new person there.
    💡 用过去式讲一个小故事：怎么被介绍／怎么开口，以及你们聊了什么。
    示例： "Last weekend I went to a friend's birthday dinner. A guy next to me said, 'Hi, I'm Tom,' and we started talking about travel. Turns out we'd both been to Japan." "At a work event last month, my colleague introduced me to her friend Mia. We hit it off right away because we're both into hiking." "I met someone new at a coffee shop yesterday. I just said, 'How's it going?' and we ended up chatting for an hour."
🎤 建议：先把对话大声朗读 2-3 遍，感受节奏。然后回答 3 个练习题并录音回听，你会发现自己哪里卡壳。

纠正提醒 · 避开这些坑

❌ 不太自然 ✅ 更自然 说明
"How to say your name?" "Hi, I'm [name]."
"My name is [name]."		 想说"我叫什么"时，直接报名字最自然，不要直译"怎么讲你的名字"。
"What is your job?" "What do you do?"
"What do you do for a living?"		 问职业用 "What do you do?" 是地道口语；"What is your job?" 语法对但很生硬。
"I'm embarrassing when I meet new people." "I get nervous / I'm shy
when I meet new people."		 embarrassing 是"让人尴尬的"，形容自己紧张该用 nervous / shy。
"I very happy to meet you." "Nice to meet you."
"Great to meet you."		 初次见面用固定说法 "Nice to meet you."，别说中式英语的 "very happy to meet you"。
"Let me introduce you my friend Tom." "Let me introduce you to
my friend Tom."		 "把 A 介绍给 B" 是 introduce A to B，别忘了介词 to。
