今日主题

购物 · 砍价与挑选

Shopping · Bargaining & Picking

逛街、逛市场、试衣服——买东西时怎么开口问价、砍价、挑尺码颜色。这5个表达+1段对话，下次出国或逛外贸店直接能用。

核心表达 · 5个高频说法（每条 3 个美国人真实日常例句）

1. "Could you give me a better price?"
能便宜点吗？/ 能不能优惠点？
使用场景在小店、市场或买贵重物品时砍价
"This jacket is nice, but could you give me a better price?"（看中夹克想砍价）
"I really like it, but it's a bit over my budget. Any discount?"（超预算，问能不能打折）
"If I buy two, can you do a better deal?"（买两件问能不能更优惠）
2. "Do you have this in another color / size?"
这个有其他颜色 / 尺码吗？
使用场景试衣服或挑东西时问有没有别的选择
"This shirt's great. Do you have it in blue?"（问有没有蓝色）
"The shoes fit, but do you have a smaller size?"（问有没有更小码）
"I love this bag. Do you have anything similar in black?"（问类似款黑色）
3. "I'm just browsing, thanks."
我就随便看看，谢谢。
使用场景导购过来问需不需要帮忙时，表示先自己逛逛
"Oh, I'm just browsing, thanks. I'll let you know if I need help."（先随便看看）
"Just looking around for now, but thanks!"（先逛着）
"I'm only window shopping today, but I appreciate it."（今天只是逛逛）
4. "Where can I find...?"
……在哪里？/ 请问……怎么走？
使用场景在超市、商场找不到某样东西，问店员
"Excuse me, where can I find the milk?"（问牛奶在哪）
"Where's the fitting room, please?"（问试衣间在哪）
"Do you know where the checkout is?"（问收银台在哪）
5. "I'll think about it."
我考虑一下。
使用场景不想当场买，礼貌地拖延 / 婉拒推销
"It's nice, but I'll think about it. Thanks anyway."（婉拒，说再想想）
"Let me sleep on it and come back later."（说回去考虑下）
"I'm not sure yet. I'll think it over."（说还没定）

情景对话 · 8轮真实聊天

🛍️ 场景：Emma 在一家服装小店看中一件夹克，和店员 Sam 一边试穿一边砍价。
Sam: Hi there! Can I help you find anything?
你好！需要我帮你找点什么吗？
Emma: I'm just browsing, thanks. I'll let you know if I need help.
我就随便看看，谢谢。需要的话我叫你。
Sam: Sure thing. Take your time.
没问题，慢慢逛。
Emma: Actually, this jacket is really nice. Do you have it in a smaller size?
其实这件夹克挺好看的，有更小一点的码吗？
Sam: Let me check... Yeah, we have it in medium. Want to try it on?
我看看……有中码。要试穿一下吗？
Emma: Yes, please. Where's the fitting room?
好啊。试衣间在哪儿？
Sam: It's right behind you, on the left.
就在你身后，左边。
Emma: Fits well! Could you give me a better price? It's a bit over my budget.
很合身！能便宜点吗？稍微超我预算了。
Sam: Hmm, I can take 10% off if you pay in cash.
嗯，你付现金的话我可以打九折。
Emma: That works for me. I'll take it.
可以，我要了。

口语练习 · 大声说出来

  1. You're at a market and want to bargain. What would you say to get a lower price?
    💡 用上今天学的 "Could you give me a better price?" / "Any discount?"
    示例： "This is nice, but it's a little pricey for me. Could you give me a better price?" "I'll take three if you can do a deal. Any discount for buying more?" "That's a bit much. Can you go lower?"
  2. A salesperson offers you help. You want to look alone first. How do you politely say you're just browsing?
    💡 用上 "I'm just browsing, thanks."
    示例： "I'm just browsing, thanks! I'll find you if I need anything." "Just looking around for now, but I appreciate the help." "Oh, I'm only window shopping today. Thank you though!"
  3. You like an item but it's not the right size or color. Ask the staff about other options.
    💡 用上 "Do you have this in...?"
    示例： "I love this. Do you have it in a larger size?" "This is great—do you have it in another color, like green?" "Do you have anything similar but in black?"
🎤 建议：把对话大声朗读 2-3 遍，感受节奏。然后回答问题并录音回听，你会发现自己哪里卡壳。

纠正提醒 · 避开这些坑

❌ 不太自然 ✅ 更自然 说明
"How much is the price?" "How much is it?"
"What's the price?"		 "How much is the price" 把 price 和 how much 说重了，啰嗦。问价直接说 how much is it。
"Give me a cheap one." "Do you have anything cheaper?" 直接说 cheap 有点冒犯店家。想找便宜的，问 "anything cheaper" 更委婉。
"I want to buy this." "I'm just browsing, thanks."
"I'm looking around."		 只是想逛、还没想买时，别说 I want to buy this（显得突兀）。婉拒推销用 just browsing。
"Where is the toilet?" "Where's the restroom?"
"Where's the fitting room?"		 在商场问洗手间/试衣间，用 restroom / fitting room 更地道；toilet 偏英式且略直白。
"Too expensive!" "It's a bit over my budget."
"That's a little pricey."		 直接喊 "too expensive" 太冲。想砍价时用 over my budget / pricey 更自然、不伤人。
