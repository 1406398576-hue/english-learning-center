Checking In & Hotel Stay

酒店入住与旅行住宿 · 从办理入住到延迟退房
"Home away from home — until checkout time."
💬 核心表达
I have a reservation under... 我有预订，名字是……
到达酒店前台的第一句话。under 后面加你的姓氏，前台系统是按姓名检索的。也可以说 I booked a room online.
影视原句
"Hi, I have a reservation under Geller. Two nights?"
— Monica Geller, Friends S09E13 / 和 Chandler 去度假时办理入住
"I have a reservation under Scott. It's a business trip."
— Michael Scott, The Office S07E04 / 出差住酒店跟前台
"We have a reservation under Pritchett. It's for three rooms."
— Jay Pritchett, Modern Family S05E13 / 全家出游订了多间房
Is breakfast included? 早餐包含在内吗？
办理入住时必问。不同酒店政策不同，有的包早餐（continental breakfast），有的要额外付费。included 是关键词。
影视原句
"Does the room rate include breakfast, or is that extra?"
— Ted Mosby, How I Met Your Mother S04E09 / 和 Barney 出差住酒店
"Is breakfast included? Because if not, I'm eating at the diner across the street."
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S07E01 / 关心早餐是否免费
"The website said breakfast was complimentary. Is that still the case?"
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S06E11 / 和前台确认网站上的承诺
Could I get a late checkout? 我可以延迟退房吗？
标准退房时间一般是上午 11 点或中午 12 点。如果你需要多睡一会儿或下午才离开，可以礼貌询问 late checkout。
影视原句
"Could we get a late checkout? Our flight isn't until 6 p.m."
— Ross Geller, Friends S10E03 / 和 Rachel 旅行想晚点退房
"Any chance I could get a late checkout? I had a rough night."
— Barney Stinson, How I Met Your Mother S05E22 / 宿醉后想多睡会儿
"I'd like to request a late checkout. Is there an extra charge for that?"
— Pam Beesly, The Office S06E16 / 和 Jim 蜜月旅行
The [something] in my room isn't working. 我房间的……坏了/不能用。
房间设施出问题时的标准说法。isn't working 比 is broken 更委婉、更常用。可替换 Wi-Fi / AC (air conditioning) / TV / shower / heater 等。
影视原句
"The Wi-Fi in my room isn't working. I have a deadline."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S08E18 / 居家办公需要网络
"The AC in my room isn't working and it's like a sauna in there."
— Marshall Eriksen, How I Met Your Mother S03E17 / 夏天空调坏了
"The TV in my room isn't working. I was gonna watch the game."
— Dwight Schrute, The Office S04E12 / 出差时电视故障
I'd like to extend my stay by one night. 我想多住一晚。
行程有变需要续住时。extend my stay 是标准说法，by one night / for two more nights 说明具体天数。
影视原句
"I'd like to extend my stay by one night. Is the room still available?"
— Rachel Green, Friends S05E22 / 旅行计划临时改变
"We need to extend our stay for two more nights. Our car broke down."
— Claire Dunphy, Modern Family S04E15 / 车坏了被迫续住
"Is it possible to extend my stay? My meeting got pushed to Friday."
— Jim Halpert, The Office S05E10 / 出差会议延期
🎭 情景对话
场景：周六下午，Emma 拖着行李箱到达一家商务酒店前台，接待员 Sarah 正在帮她办理入住手续，两人聊到了早餐和房间设施。
Emma
Hi, I have a reservation under Miller. I booked it online last week.
你好，我有预订，名字是 Miller。我上周在网上订的。
Sarah
Let me check... Yes, here it is. Emma Miller, two nights, standard king room. Could I see your ID and credit card, please?
我查一下……是的，找到了。Emma Miller，两晚，标准大床房。请出示一下您的身份证件和信用卡。
Emma
Sure, here you go. Is breakfast included in the rate?
好的，给您。早餐包含在房费里吗？
Sarah
Yes, continental breakfast is included. It's served from 6:30 to 10 a.m. on the second floor. Your room is 412, fourth floor. Here's your key card.
是的，包含欧式早餐。供应时间是早上 6:30 到 10 点，在二楼。您的房间是 412，四楼。这是您的房卡。
Emma
Great, thanks. One more thing — what's the checkout time?
太好了，谢谢。还有一件事——退房时间是几点？
Sarah
Checkout is at noon. If you need a late checkout, just let us know the night before and we can usually arrange until 1 p.m. at no extra charge.
中午 12 点退房。如果您需要延迟退房，提前一晚告诉我们，通常可以免费延到下午 1 点。
Emma
Good to know. Also, I noticed the gym on the website. Is it open 24 hours?
知道了。另外，我在网站上看到这里有健身房。是 24 小时开放的吗？
Sarah
Yes, the fitness center is open 24/7. You'll need your key card to access it. If you need anything else, just dial 0 from your room phone. Enjoy your stay!
是的，健身中心全天候开放。您需要用房卡刷卡进入。如果还需要什么，从房间电话拨 0 就行。祝您入住愉快！
🎤 口语练习
1 You just arrived at a hotel. Walk up to the front desk and tell them you have a reservation. Ask if breakfast is included.
你可以这样说
A："Hi, I have a reservation under Smith. I booked it through the website. Is breakfast included in the room rate?"
B："Hello, I'm checking in. My name is Li, and I have a reservation for two nights. Does that come with breakfast?"
C："Good afternoon. I believe I have a booking here — under the name Chen. Could you confirm if breakfast is included?"
2 You're in your hotel room and the Wi-Fi isn't working. Call the front desk and explain the problem. Ask if someone can come take a look.
你可以这样说
A："Hi, this is room 412. The Wi-Fi in my room isn't working. Could someone come take a look? I need it for work."
B："Front desk? I'm in room 205. The internet keeps dropping and I can't connect. Is there someone who can help with that?"
C："Hello, I have a small issue — the Wi-Fi in my room doesn't seem to be working. Could you send someone up to check it? I'm in 318."
3 Your flight got cancelled and you need to stay one more night. Go to the front desk and ask if you can extend your stay. Ask about a late checkout too.
你可以这样说
A："Hi, my flight got cancelled so I'd like to extend my stay by one night. Is my room still available? And could I get a late checkout tomorrow?"
B："I need to stay an extra night because of a flight delay. Can I extend my reservation? Also, what time is checkout, and is a late checkout possible?"
C："My travel plans changed and I need to stay until Thursday. I'd like to extend my stay for one more night if the room isn't booked. And could I request a late checkout?"
⚠️ 纠正提醒
"I want to open a room."
"I'd like to check in." / "I have a reservation."
中文说「开间房」，但英语里没有 open a room 的说法。check in 才是办理入住的标准表达，前台一听就懂。
"My room has no Wi-Fi."
"The Wi-Fi in my room isn't working." / "I can't get Wi-Fi in my room."
has no Wi-Fi 暗示房间本来就没有，但酒店通常都有。isn't working 表示「坏了/连不上」，更准确地描述问题，也更容易获得帮助。
"I want to live one more day."
"I'd like to extend my stay by one night." / "Can I stay one more night?"
live 指「居住/生活」，在酒店语境里不自然。extend my stay 是酒店行业的标准说法，by one night 说明具体延长多久。
"I booked a room. My name is Li."
"I have a reservation under Li." / "I booked a room under the name Li."
单独说 I booked a room 没错，但前台需要按姓名查系统。under + 姓氏是酒店前台最熟悉的表达方式，效率更高。
"Can you change my room?"
"Could I switch to a different room?" / "Would it be possible to change rooms?"
Can you change my room? 语法上说得通，但听起来像命令。Could I switch to...? 更礼貌、更地道，switch 在换房语境里比 change 更常用。
