Opening a Bank Account

银行开户 · 从办卡到问费用的真实表达
"I just need a place to keep my money where I won't spend it all on coffee."
💬 核心表达
I'd like to open a checking account. 我想开一个活期/支票账户。
走进银行对柜员说的第一句话。checking account 是美国最常用的日常账户（随时存取、可刷卡），savings account 则是储蓄账户。不用 "I want to make a bank card"，老外开户开口会说 open an account，卡只是附带。
影视原句
"I'd like to open a checking account. And maybe a savings account, too. You know, for all the money I'm about to have."
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S02E12 / 误以为中了彩票后跑去银行
"I'd like to open a checking account. A joint one. With my cat. His name is Sprinkles and he has no credit history, so I understand if there's a process."
— Angela Martin, The Office S04E04 / 在柜台一本正经地提出离谱需求
"I'd like to open a checking account for my son. He's ten, but he's already showing signs of fiscal responsibility — mostly because I told him I'll cut off his allowance."
— Claire Dunphy, Modern Family S06E08 / 带孩子去银行开户学理财
What do I need to bring in? 我需要带什么材料来？
打电话或线上预约开户前，先问清楚要带什么证件。美国银行通常需要 photo ID（驾照/护照）、Social Security Number（SSN）或 ITIN、proof of address（地址证明，如水电账单）。这个问法比 "What materials do I need?" 自然一万倍。
影视原句
"What do I need to bring in to open an account? Just my ID? Because my ID photo is from a very dark period in my life and I'd like to prepare you."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S05E17 / 打电话咨询开户材料
"What do I need to bring in? My birth certificate? My firstborn? My soul? Because I've seen how banks work in movies."
— Marshall Eriksen, How I Met Your Mother S03E10 / 夸张地问开户材料
"What do I need to bring in to prove I live here? I have a utility bill, a lease, and a very angry letter from my HOA. Will any of those work?"
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S05E14 / 询问地址证明要求
Is there a monthly maintenance fee? 有月管理费吗？
开户时一定要确认的费用。maintenance fee 是账户月费，很多银行只要保持最低余额（minimum balance）或直接存款（direct deposit）就能免除。还有 overdraft fee（透支费）、ATM fee（跨行取现手续费）也可以顺便问。
影视原句
"Is there a monthly maintenance fee? Because I'm trying to save money, not enter a subscription service I didn't ask for."
— Monica Geller, Friends S08E03 / 仔细核对开户费用
"Is there a monthly maintenance fee? And if so, can I pay it in beets? I have a lot of beets."
— Dwight Schrute, The Office S06E12 / 用甜菜试图抵账
"Is there a monthly maintenance fee on this account? My husband opens things and never reads the fine print, so I'm the one who has to ask."
— Claire Dunphy, Modern Family S04E19 / 替家里管钱的人仔细问
Can I set up online banking? 我能开通网上银行吗？
开户时或开完户后一定会问。set up 是"设置/开通"，比 open online banking 自然。也可以问 Can I get the mobile app? / How do I enroll in online banking? 现在很多银行开户当天就帮你把网银和手机 App 一起设好。
影视原句
"Can I set up online banking? I don't trust the internet, but I also don't trust myself to remember to go to a physical building."
— Ross Geller, Friends S09E14 / 半信半疑地想开通网银
"Can I set up online banking? I need to be able to check my balance at 3 AM while questioning every decision I've ever made."
— Pam Beesly, The Office S05E22 / 半夜查账的真实需求
"Can I set up online banking, mobile alerts, and maybe an app that yells at me when I spend too much? Does that exist?"
— Lily Aldrin, How I Met Your Mother S04E16 / 想开通各类提醒功能
I'd like to deposit / withdraw some cash. 我想存 / 取点现金。
开户后日常使用银行时的高频句。deposit = 存，withdraw = 取。柜台说法：I'd like to make a deposit / I'd like to make a withdrawal。ATM 也可以直接说 I'm here to deposit some cash。注意 withdraw 的重音在第二个音节。
影视原句
"I'd like to deposit some cash. It's all in singles. From tips. I'm a dancer. A tap dancer. Why are you looking at me like that?"
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S06E09 / 拿小费去存钱时的尴尬解释
"I'd like to withdraw some cash. Enough for a wedding ring. Don't worry, she said yes — I just forgot to get the ring part handled."
— Jim Halpert, The Office S03E23 / 取钱买婚戒

— Mitchell Pritchett, Modern Family S07E05 / 去银行存母亲给的支票
🎭 情景对话
场景：Alex 刚到美国工作，周五午休去银行开户。银行柜员 Sarah 协助办理。
Alex
Hi, I'd like to open a checking account, please.
你好，我想开一个活期账户。
Sarah
Of course. Do you have an appointment, or are you walking in today?
好的。您有预约吗，还是今天直接来的？
Alex
I'm walking in. I actually called yesterday and asked what I need to bring in — I have my passport, my work visa, and a utility bill with my address.
直接来的。我昨天打电话问过要带什么材料——我有护照、工作签证，还有一张写了地址的水电费账单。
Sarah
Perfect, that covers everything. This account has no monthly maintenance fee if you keep a minimum balance of $500, or if you set up direct deposit with your employer.
太好了，材料齐全。这个账户只要保持 500 美元最低余额，或者设置工资直接存入，就没有月管理费。
Alex
Great, my company can do direct deposit. And can I set up online banking today too?
太好了，我公司可以直接存工资。那今天能一起开通网上银行吗？
Sarah
Absolutely. I'll help you create your username and set up the mobile app before you leave. You'll also get a debit card in the mail within 5 to 7 business days.
当然可以。您离开前我会帮您创建用户名并设置好手机 App。借记卡会在 5 到 7 个工作日内寄到。
Alex
Got it. One more thing — I'd like to deposit $800 in cash to get started.
明白了。还有一件事——我想先存 800 美元现金进去。
Sarah
No problem. I'll process the deposit right after we finish the paperwork. You're all set!
没问题。我们填完资料后我马上帮您存。都办好了！
🎤 口语练习
1假设你刚搬到一个新城市，去银行开户。用英语说明你想开一个 checking account，并询问需要带什么材料。
示例回答
Hi, I'd like to open a checking account. I just moved here last week. What do I need to bring in — just my passport and proof of address?
I need to open a checking account for my salary. Could you tell me what documents I need to bring?
I'd like to open an account, but I'm not a U.S. citizen. What ID do I need to bring in?
2柜员向你介绍账户有月管理费，但可以通过 direct deposit 免除。用英语复述你理解了规则，并同意开通。
示例回答
So if I set up direct deposit with my employer, there's no monthly fee, right? That works for me.
Got it — no monthly maintenance fee as long as my paycheck goes directly into the account. I'll sign up for that.
Direct deposit sounds good. My company already offers it, so I don't think I'll have to pay the monthly fee.
3开户后你想存一笔现金，并开通手机银行。用英语一次性表达这两个需求。
示例回答
I'd also like to deposit $500 in cash, and can you help me set up online banking on my phone?
Before I leave, I'd like to make a cash deposit and download the mobile banking app. Is that okay?
I brought some cash to deposit. And could you walk me through setting up the app so I can check my balance?
✅ 纠正提醒
I want to make a bank card.
I'd like to open a checking account.
"办卡" 在中文里很自然，但英文里开户叫 open an account，银行卡只是附带的 debit card。直接说 make a card 老外会愣一下。
How much is the monthly fee?
Is there a monthly maintenance fee?
问有没有月费，用 Is there...? 更地道；maintenance fee 是银行术语，"账户维护费"。How much 更适合已经确定有费用后再追问金额。
I want to save money to the bank.
I'd like to make a deposit.
"存钱" 不是 save money to the bank（save 指省下/储蓄这个行为）。在银行柜台说 deposit，日常也可以说 put money into my account。
Please give me my money.
I'd like to withdraw some cash, please.
取钱时说 give me my money 听起来像抢银行。礼貌说法是 withdraw cash / make a withdrawal，并说明金额。
Open the online bank.
Can I set up online banking?
"网上银行" 是 online banking（名词短语），不是 online bank。开通/设置用 set up 或 enroll in。
