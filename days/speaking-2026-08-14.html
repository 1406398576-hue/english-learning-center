Moving & Renting an Apartment

搬家租房 · 看房、签约、搬进来的真实表达
"Home is where the boxes are."
💬 核心表达
I'm looking for a place to rent. 我在找房子租
告诉朋友、中介或房东你正在租房，比 I want to rent a house 更自然、更口语。place 是万能词，泛指住处。
影视原句
"I'm looking for a place to rent. Something small, with decent light."
— Monica Geller, Friends S06E12 / 和 Rachel 聊换大房子时
"I'm trying to find a place closer to work. The commute is killing me."
— Jim Halpert, The Office S03E18 / 考虑搬去 Stamford 附近
"We're looking for a place with an extra room for the baby."
— Claire Dunphy, Modern Family S04E03 / 全家讨论换房
Is it still available? 这套房子还在租吗？
看到租房广告后打电话或发消息的第一句。available 表示「可租/在售」，是租房市场高频词。
影视原句
"Hi, I saw the ad online. Is it still available?"
— Rachel Green, Friends S02E10 / 打电话问公寓广告
"Is the apartment still available? I'd love to take a look."
— Pam Beesly, The Office S05E14 / 和中介通话
"Is this room still available? My friend needs a place ASAP."
— Ted Mosby, How I Met Your Mother S01E18 / 帮 Marshall 找房
Are utilities included? 水电煤气费包含在房租里吗？
看房时必问。utilities 指水电煤、网络等公用事业费，决定你每月真实开销。
影视原句
"Does the rent include utilities, or do I pay those separately?"
— Monica Geller, Friends S07E15 / 和房东谈条件
"Are utilities included? Because that changes everything."
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S02E11 / 看房时算总成本
"Is water and gas included, or just electricity?"
— Lily Aldrin, How I Met Your Mother S03E04 / 和房东确认细节
I'd like to move in on... 我想在……搬进来
决定租房后，用来和房东确认入住时间。move in 是「搬入」，move out 是「搬出」。
影视原句
"I'd like to move in on the first of next month if possible."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S05E07 / 签新公寓合同时
"We were hoping to move in by the end of the week."
— Cameron Tucker, Modern Family S03E22 / 和房产经纪交涉
"Can I move in this weekend? My lease is up on Friday."
— Marshall Eriksen, How I Met Your Mother S02E15 / 赶时间找新住处
I need to give my landlord notice. 我要提前通知房东（我要搬走了）
换房时必须提前通知现任房东，美国常见 30 天。give notice 是正式但口语化的说法。
影视原句
"I need to give my landlord notice before I can sign anything."
— Rachel Green, Friends S05E16 / 想搬去离 Ross 近一点的地方
"My lease isn't up yet. I have to put in my notice first."
— Jim Halpert, The Office S03E20 / 解释搬家的麻烦
"We still need to give our landlord 30 days' notice."
— Mitchell Pritchett, Modern Family S05E08 / 计划搬进新房前
🎭 情景对话
场景：周五下午，Alex 跟着房东 Mr. Patterson 看一间一居室公寓，两人边走边聊租金、家具和入住时间。
Alex
Hi, I'm Alex. Thanks for showing me the place.
你好，我是 Alex。谢谢你带我看房。
Mr. Patterson
Nice to meet you. So, you're looking for a place to rent?
很高兴见到你。所以你在找房子租？
Alex
Yeah, something close to downtown. Is it still available?
对，最好离市中心近一点。这套还在租吗？
Mr. Patterson
It is. The last tenant moved out last weekend. How soon are you looking to move in?
还在。上一个租客上周末刚搬走。你想多久搬进来？
Alex
I'd like to move in on September 1st if possible. My current lease is up at the end of the month.
如果可能的话，我想 9 月 1 号搬进来。我现在的租约月底到期。
Mr. Patterson
That works for me. The rent is $1,200 a month. Are utilities included?
可以。房租每月 1200 美元。水电煤包含吗？
Alex
Good question. I noticed the place is furnished. Is that extra?
问得好。我看这里带家具，这要额外加钱吗？
Mr. Patterson
No, the furniture stays. Utilities are separate, though — usually around $80 a month. If you want it, I just need first month's rent plus deposit.
不用，家具会留下。但水电费另算，大概每月 80 美元。如果你要租，我只需要首月租金加押金。
Alex
Sounds good. I just need to give my landlord notice first, but I'm definitely interested.
听起来不错。我得先通知我现在的房东，但我确实很有兴趣。
🎤 口语练习
1 You found an apartment listing online. Call the landlord and ask if it's still available and when you can see it.
你可以这样说
A："Hi, I saw your listing online. Is it still available? I'd love to come see it this weekend."
B："Hello, I'm calling about the apartment on Maple Street. Is it still on the market? When can I take a look?"
C："Hi there, is the one-bedroom still available? I'm free tomorrow afternoon if that works for a viewing."
2 You're viewing an apartment. Ask what's included in the rent and whether it's furnished.
你可以这样说
A："Are utilities included, or are they separate? And is the place furnished?"
B："What's included in the rent? Does it come with internet and water, or do I pay those myself?"
C："Is it furnished? If not, can I move my own stuff in before the first?"
3 You decided to take the apartment. Tell the landlord when you want to move in and mention you need to give notice first.
你可以这样说
A："I'd like to move in on the first of next month. I just need to give my current landlord 30 days' notice."
B："I'm definitely in. I'd like to move in on September 1st, but I need to put in my notice first."
C："This works for me. I can move in at the end of the month. I'll let my landlord know today."
⚠️ 纠正提醒
"I want to rent a house."
"I'm looking for a place to rent." / "I'm trying to find an apartment."
直接说 I want to rent a house 像课本句子。口语里更常说 look for a place，place 可以指公寓、房子、单间，灵活自然。
"The rent is how much?"
"How much is the rent?" / "What's the monthly rent?"
中文习惯说「租金是多少？」，但英语里疑问词要前置。How much is the rent? 最自然，monthly rent 更清楚。
"The house has furniture."
"It's furnished." / "It comes furnished." / "It's unfurnished."
租房语境里有现成的形容词 furnished / unfurnished，比 has furniture 地道得多。comes furnished 强调「包含家具」。
"I will move next week."
"I'm moving next week." / "I'm moving in next week."
谈已经安排好的搬家，用现在进行时表将来更自然。move 单独用指「搬家」，move in 强调「搬入」。
"I want to live near subway."
"I'd like to be close to the subway." / "I'm looking for somewhere near public transit."
near subway 少了冠词，应为 near the subway。另外 close to / near public transit 更口语，subway 在美国不每个城市都有，transit 更通用。
