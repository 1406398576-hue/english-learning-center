餐厅点餐：点菜、问推荐、加配菜、买单

Ordering at a Restaurant

在国外吃饭，从进门到买单全程都要开口。今天这 5 个表达帮你搞定点餐最核心的环节——要什么、问推荐、问配菜、最后买单，不用背菜单也能从容点餐。

核心表达 · 5个高频说法（每条 3 句真实美剧原句）

1. "Could I get...?"
我想要…… / 能给我来一份……吗？（点餐最自然的说法）
使用场景点菜、点饮料时，比 I want 更礼貌自然
《Friends》Joey 在咖啡馆点餐时"Could I get a pepperoni pizza, extra cheese?"
《The Office》Michael 请大家吃饭时"Could I get a burger and fries, please?"
《Modern Family》Phil 在餐厅点早餐时"Could I get the pancakes with a side of bacon?"
🎬 真实场景：服务员走过来问 "What can I get you?" 你回 "Could I get the chicken sandwich?" 就够了——不用纠结语法，这句话在美国餐厅每天说几百万次。
2. "I'll have the..."
我要一份……（点菜的经典说法，简洁明确）
使用场景已经看好菜单，直接告诉服务员要哪道菜
《Friends》Monica 在餐厅点菜时"I'll have the chef's salad, please."
《How I Met Your Mother》Ted 在牛排馆点餐"I'll have the steak, medium rare."
《The Office》Jim 在午餐时"I'll have the turkey club, no mayo."
🎬 真实场景：I'll have 和 Could I get 可以互换，但 I'll have 更干脆，适合你已经决定好了的时候。加 "medium rare / well done" 说明熟度更地道。
3. "What do you recommend?"
你有什么推荐的？/ 这儿什么好吃？
使用场景第一次来、看不懂菜单或拿不定主意时问服务员
《Friends》Rachel 在高级餐厅看菜单时"This all looks amazing. What do you recommend?"
《Modern Family》Jay 第一次去新餐厅"What do you recommend? What's good here?"
《How I Met Your Mother》Marshall 饿坏了时"I'm starving — what do you recommend?"
🎬 真实场景：第一次来一家餐厅，别只盯着菜单纠结，直接问 "What do you recommend?" 服务员通常很乐意告诉你招牌菜。
4. "Does this come with...?"
这个配……吗？/ 这个带……吗？（问配菜、附赠）
使用场景想知道某道菜是否自带配菜（薯条、沙拉、饮料等）
《Friends》Monica 问服务员配菜时"Does the salmon come with a side salad?"
《Modern Family》Claire 给家人点餐时"Does the kids' meal come with a drink?"
《The Office》Dwight 在三明治店"Does the sandwich come with chips or fries?"
🎬 真实场景：美国餐厅的主菜通常不含配菜，问一句 "Does this come with fries?" 可以避免上菜后才发现什么都没有的尴尬。
5. "Could we get the check, please?"
麻烦买单 / 可以结账吗？
使用场景吃完准备走，叫服务员过来结账
《Friends》Chandler 吃完饭准备走时"Excuse me, could we get the check, please?"
《How I Met Your Mother》Barney 常说的经典"Check, please."
《The Office》Michael 请完客后"Alright, could we get the check? I'll take care of it."
🎬 真实场景：美国餐厅不会主动送账单，你要主动叫。"Check, please." 是最短版本；想礼貌点就说 "Could we get the check, please?"

情景对话 · 真实餐厅点餐（8 轮）

🍽️ 场景：Emma 和 Tom 第一次来一家餐厅吃午饭，从看菜单、问推荐、点餐、问配菜，到吃完买单。
Emma: This place looks great. Have you been here before?
这地方看着不错。你以前来过吗？
Tom: No, but I've heard good things. What do you recommend?
没，但我听说挺好的。你有什么推荐？
Waiter: Hi there! Can I get you something to drink first?
你好！先来点什么喝的吗？
Emma: Could I get an iced tea, please? And I think we're ready to order.
能给我来一杯冰茶吗？另外我们应该可以点菜了。
Tom: I'll have the grilled salmon. Does it come with a side?
我要一份烤三文鱼。这个带配菜吗？
Waiter: It comes with your choice of rice or roasted vegetables.
可以选米饭或烤蔬菜作为配菜。
Emma: I'll have the same, with the vegetables. That was a good call, Tom.
我也来一份一样的，配蔬菜。选得好，Tom。
Tom: That was delicious. Excuse me, could we get the check, please? I'll get it this time.
真好吃。打扰一下，麻烦买单？这次我来请。

口语练习 · 大声说出来

  1. You walk into a restaurant and the waiter asks "What can I get you?" Order a main dish and a drink.
    💡 用 "Could I get..." 或 "I'll have the..." 点菜，加一句饮料。
    示例： "Could I get the chicken sandwich and a coke, please?" "I'll have the steak, medium rare, and an iced tea." "Could I get the pasta with a side salad, and a coffee?"
  2. You're looking at the menu but can't decide. Ask the waiter for a recommendation.
    💡 用 "What do you recommend?" 开头，可以加一句 "What's good here?"
    示例： "This is my first time here. What do you recommend?" "I'm not sure yet — what do you recommend? What's good here?" "What do you recommend? I'm in the mood for something light."
  3. You finished your meal and want to pay. Politely ask for the check and offer to pay.
    💡 用 "Could we get the check, please?" 再加一句 "I'll get this one."
    示例： "Excuse me, could we get the check, please? I'll take care of it." "Check, please. I'll get this one — you got the last one." "Could we get the check? It's on me this time."
🎤 建议：先把对话大声朗读 2–3 遍找节奏，注意 "Could I get" 连读成 /kʊd aɪ ɡɛt/，"I'll have the" 连读成 /aɪl hæv ðə/。然后关掉中文，自己用英语回答 3 个练习并录音。

纠正提醒 · 避开这些坑

❌ 不太自然 ✅ 更自然 说明
"I want this one." "I'll have the..."
"Could I get the..."		 I want 语气太直白，像命令；美国人在餐厅几乎不说 I want，用 I'll have 或 Could I get 更自然礼貌。
"Give me a coffee." "Could I get a coffee?"
"I'll have a coffee."		 Give me 是祈使句，听起来粗鲁；改成 Could I get 或 I'll have，加个 please 更好。
"How much is it?"（还在吃饭时） "Could we get the check, please?" 还在吃饭时问价格不自然；吃完想走直接要账单。美国餐厅不主动送账单，需要你叫。
"I want to pay." "I'll get this one."
"It's on me."		 I want to pay 太生硬；朋友之间抢着买单用 I'll get this one 或 It's on me 更地道。
"What is your recommendation?" "What do you recommend?"
"What's good here?"		 What is your recommendation 太书面；口语里直接用动词 recommend，或者随口问 What's good here? 更自然。
