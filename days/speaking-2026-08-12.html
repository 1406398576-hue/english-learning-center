Introducing Yourself

自我介绍 · 让别人记住你
"You never get a second chance to make a first impression."
💬 核心表达
I'm from… originally 我老家是……（但现在住别处）
介绍家乡 + 说明搬来本地多久。用 originally 顺手交代"现在不住那儿了"。比直接说 I come from… 更自然。
影视原句
"I'm from Long Island."
— Monica Geller, Friends / 反复出现（Ross & Monica 的家乡梗）
"I'm from Scranton, born and raised."
— Michael Scott, The Office S04E01 / 介绍自己时的标志性骄傲口吻
"I mean, I'm from Ohio. We don't just blurt things out like that."
— Ted Mosby, How I Met Your Mother S01E01 / 向 Robin 解释自己说话没那么直接
I work as a… / I do… for a living 我是做……工作的
非正式场合自然说自己的职业。I do… for a living 比 My job is… 更像真人说话。别用 I am a + 职位长句，保持短。
影视原句
"I'm an actor."
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends / Joey 的标志性自我介绍，简单直接
"I'm the assistant to the regional manager."
— Dwight Schrute, The Office / 强调 assistant to（不是 assistant）
"I'm a realtor."
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S01E01 / 全家自我介绍时 Phil 的招牌身份
I've been getting into… lately 最近迷上了……
聊兴趣爱好的自然开头。get into 比 like 更有「主动投入」的意味，lately 让整个表达更随意。
影视原句
"I've been getting into yoga. It's really helped with my stress."
— Phoebe Buffay, Friends S05 / Phoebe 给朋友安利瑜伽时的语境
"I have been getting into cooking lately. You know, watching those cooking shows?"
— Lily Aldrin, How I Met Your Mother S03 / Lily 搬家后培养新爱好
"I've really been getting into this whole photography thing."
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S02 / Phil 典型的"三分钟热度"式表达
I'm kind of a… person 我算是那种……的人
用 kind of 软化语气，不给自己贴死标签。形容性格/习惯的最佳句式，比 I am a… person 更口语化。
影视原句
"I'm kind of a neat freak."
— Monica Geller, Friends / Monica 对自己洁癖的清醒认知
"I'm kind of a cool dad. That's my thing."
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S01E01 / Phil 的经典自我介绍
"I'm a bit of a hopeless romantic, I'll admit it."
— Ted Mosby, How I Met Your Mother S01E01 / 向 Robin 坦承自己
Great to finally meet you! / I've heard so much about you 终于见到你了！/ 久仰大名
初次见面「果然是你」的暖场句。finally 暗示之前听过对方，一下就拉近距离。heard so much 是最安全的破冰客套。
影视原句
"Oh, it's so nice to finally meet you! Joey talks about you all the time."
— Rachel Green, Friends S02 / Rachel 第一次见 Joey 的约会对象
"Hi, nice to meet you. I've heard a lot about you."
— Pam Beesly, The Office S03 / Pam 见新同事时的招牌羞涩开场
"I've heard so much about you!"
— Ted Mosby, How I Met Your Mother S01 / 被介绍给 Barney 新女友时的标准社交句
🎭 情景对话
场景：周五晚上的科技行业 happy hour。Sam（设计师，芝加哥人）和 Jamie（内容营销，奥斯汀人）第一次见面。
Sam
Hey, is this seat taken?
嘿，这位置有人吗？
Jamie
No, go ahead! I'm Jamie, by the way.
没人，坐吧！对了，我是 Jamie。
Sam
Sam. Great to meet you.
我是 Sam，很高兴认识你。
Jamie
So, what do you do, Sam?
那，你是做什么的？
Sam
I'm a UX designer. Been doing this for about four years. What about you?
我是做 UX 设计的，干这行差不多四年了。你呢？
Jamie
Oh nice! I do content marketing. Are you from around here?
哇不错！我做内容营销的。你是本地人吗？
Sam
I'm from Chicago, originally — moved here about a year ago for this job.
老家芝加哥的，大概一年前为了这份工作搬过来的。
Jamie
I'm from Austin myself. Been here two years now. What do you do outside of work?
我是奥斯汀的，来这儿两年了。你工作之外喜欢做什么？
Sam
I've been getting into hiking lately. There's so many trails around here. I'm kind of an outdoors person, I guess.
最近迷上了徒步，这边步道真多啊。我大概算是户外型的吧。
Jamie
Oh, I've been meaning to try that! I'm more of a gym person, honestly. We should totally go sometime!
我一直想去试试！老实说，我更多是去健身房那种。改天可以一起去啊！
🎤 口语练习
1 Introduce yourself to someone at a networking event. Say your name, where you're from, and what you do.
你可以这样说
A："Hey, I'm Li Wei. I'm from Shenzhen, originally, but I've been living here for three years now. I work as a product manager."
B："Hi, I'm Wei. I'm from the south of China — moved up here for work. I do graphic design."
C："Oh hey, I'm Li Wei. I'm a software engineer. I grew up in Shenzhen and I've been with my current company for two years."
2 Tell someone what you do for a living and one thing you actually enjoy about it.
你可以这样说
A："I do digital marketing. What I really like about it is that every day is different — you're never doing the same thing twice."
B："I'm a high school teacher. It's exhausting sometimes, but honestly, the kids make it worth it."
C："I work as a data analyst. I know, that sounds boring — but I love it. It's like solving puzzles all day."
3 Describe your personality in 2-3 sentences. Are you more of an introvert or an extrovert? Use "I'm kind of a… person".
你可以这样说
A："I'm kind of an introvert. I need my alone time to recharge. But once I get comfortable, I can talk for hours."
B："I'm more of a people person, honestly. I get my energy from being around others. I'm kind of the loud one in my friend group."
C："I'd say I'm somewhere in the middle. I'm kind of a planner — I like to have things figured out — but I'm also pretty easygoing when plans change."
⚠️ 纠正提醒
"My name is Zhang Wei. I am come from Beijing."
"Hey, I'm Wei. I'm from Beijing, originally."
中文「我叫……我来自……」直译显得僵硬。英语自我介绍直接用 I'm + 名字，I'm from + 地点。originally 放在句末或句中，暗示你现在不住那儿，比单独一句 Born in… 更自然。
"My job is a designer." / "My job is marketing."
"I'm a designer." / "I do marketing." / "I work in marketing."
英语里很少说 My job is a + 职位（这等于在说"我的工作这个人是个设计师"）。正确结构：I'm a + 职业 / I work as a + 职业 / I do + 领域。
"I very like hiking." / "I very much like sports."
"I really like hiking." / "I'm really into hiking." / "I've been getting into hiking lately."
very 不直接修饰动词。very like 是中式英语经典坑。用 really / totally / super 修饰 like/love，或者直接用 I'm into / I'm a big fan of 更地道。
"I am a careful person." / "I am an active person."
"I'm kind of a detail-oriented person." / "I'm more of an outdoors person."
careful 在英语里偏「小心翼翼的」，不是中文「细心」的意思。同理 active person 太笼统。用 kind of / more of 软化语气，用更具体的形容词（detail-oriented、outdoorsy、people person、homebody 等）。
"Nice to meet you." 在对话结尾说
开头说 "Nice to meet you." / 结尾说 "Nice meeting you." 或 "It was great talking to you."
Nice to meet you 是见面时的固定表达。对话结束时要说 Nice meeting you（用 -ing 形式），或用 "Great talking to you" / "Let's keep in touch" 自然收尾。这个小细节老外一听就知道你是不是真的会说。
