At the Supermarket

超市购物 · 从找东西到结账的真实表达
"I only came in for milk. How is my cart this full?"
💬 核心表达
Where would I find the...? 我在哪能找到……？
在超市里找不到东西时，问店员最自然的开场白。用 would 而不是 where is，语气更委婉——你不是在质问，而是在礼貌求助。也可以换一种问法：What aisle is the [东西] in?（在哪条过道）。超市员工一听就知道你想去哪儿，通常直接给你指方向。
影视原句
"Where would I find the capers? I've been up and down every aisle twice. This place is a maze — I just wanted to make one potato salad."
— Monica Geller, Friends S04E12 / 为感恩节大餐采购时在超市迷路
"Where would I find the fancy cheese? The kind that looks like it judges you? I need it for the dinner party that will make Jan weep with joy."
— Michael Scott, The Office S03E05 / 为办晚宴采购被琳琅满目的奶酪搞晕
"Where would I find the artisanal bread flour? Claire said 'just grab some,' but there are forty kinds of flour here and I'm having an existential crisis in aisle seven."
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S08E04 / 被妻子派来买菜结果选择困难
Do you guys carry...? 你们有卖……吗？
问超市有没有某类商品。carry = 经销/出售（商品），是零售业最地道的动词，比 sell 自然得多。you guys 是口语中跟店员打招呼最常见的方式——不分男女，在美国超市天天能听到。也可以缩成 Do you carry...? 但加了 guys 更随意亲切。
影视原句
"Do you guys carry those little marshmallows? The ones you put in hot chocolate? I'm making a 'Joey Special' and it's not special without them — it's just sad cocoa."
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S07E15 / 为自制热可可找配料
"Do you carry fresh beets? Not canned. I grow my own, obviously, but the crop was... compromised. By a bear. Long story."
— Dwight Schrute, The Office S05E09 / 在超市找甜菜时的离谱解释
"Do you guys carry real Colombian coffee? Not that brown water you call 'breakfast blend.' A man shouldn't have to explain himself at 7 AM."
— Jay Pritchett, Modern Family S03E11 / 对超市咖啡的日常挑剔
Is this on sale? 这个在打折吗？
看到货架上的东西但不确定价格是否优惠时问。on sale 在美国口语里 = 在促销/打折，不是中文"在出售"的意思（那是 for sale）。超市里促销牌和价签经常对不上，所以这句在超市特别高频。也可以问 Is there a sale on this? 或 Does the discount still apply?
影视原句
"Is this on sale? Because the sign said 'low price' but the tag says otherwise, and I need to know before I commit emotionally to this cheese."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S05E14 / 对着一块奶酪纠结价格的自我调侃
"Is this on sale? The sign is three aisles over and I refuse to pay full price for something that is clearly being promoted. Check the system."
— Angela Martin, The Office S06E08 / 超市精打细算到较真的程度
"Is this on sale? I have a coupon, a digital coupon, and a loyalty discount — I need to know the order of operations before this gets mathematically complicated."
— Claire Dunphy, Modern Family S06E16 / 优惠券叠加达人的购物时刻
I brought my own bags. 我自己带袋子了。
结账时告诉收银员你自带了购物袋。美国很多州限塑（比如加州、纽约州），自带袋子非常普遍——有些店甚至对塑料袋收费。这句话一到收银台就能用上。如果不想用袋子可以简单说 I don't need a bag, thanks。比 give me a bag 礼貌且环保得多。
影视原句
"I brought my own bags. They're made from recycled sailboat sails. The ocean gave them to me, and now they hold my kale. It's a beautiful cycle."
— Phoebe Buffay, Friends S08E06 / 带着自制环保袋购物的经典 Phoebe 式发言
"I don't need a bag. I have a system. Everything fits in this rucksack by weight category. Bags are for people who lack preparation."
— Dwight Schrute, The Office S07E02 / 拒绝超市塑料袋的 Dwight 式自信
"I brought my own bags — monogrammed, obviously. If you're saving the planet, you might as well do it with a little flair."
— Cameron Tucker, Modern Family S09E05 / 带着定制环保袋购物
That's everything for me. 我就买这些了（没有了）。
收银员扫完码问你"还有别的吗 / 就这些？"时回应。that's everything 和 that'll be all 都是超市结账最标准的说法，带着"我买完了，可以结账了"的意思。比 I don't want anything else 或 I finish 自然得多。这句话几乎是每趟超市购物的收尾必备。
影视原句
"That's everything for me. And if the total is under fifty dollars, it's a miracle. I only came in for milk — how did I end up with a cart full of cheese?"
— Rachel Green, Friends S03E07 / 结账时感叹只买牛奶却买了一车
"That'll be all. And I expect the total to be exactly as calculated — I brought my own scale. Don't look at me like that."
— Dwight Schrute, The Office S06E14 / 结账时掏出自制秤
"That's everything. And please don't put the eggs at the bottom — in my country, we respect the eggs. They go on top, like royalty."
— Gloria Pritchett, Modern Family S04E09 / 结账时叮嘱收银员善待鸡蛋
🎭 情景对话
场景：周六上午，Alex 在大型超市采购一周食材，找不到几样东西。正在理货的店员 Maria 主动询问，最后 Alex 到收银台结账。
Maria
Hey, finding everything okay?
嘿，都找得到吗？
Alex
Almost. Where would I find the pasta sauce? I've been up and down the Italian aisle but I can only find the glass jars.
差不多了。我在哪能找到意面酱？我在意大利食品那条过道来回走了两趟，只看到玻璃罐装的。
Maria
Oh, the canned kind? That's actually in aisle 7 with the canned goods. Were you looking for a specific brand?
哦，罐装的那种？那个其实在第7过道，罐头食品区。你在找特定牌子吗？
Alex
Yeah, do you guys carry the San Marzano one? My friend swears by it and I said I'd pick some up.
对，你们有卖 San Marzano 那个牌子吗？我朋友极力推荐，我说我来买点。
Maria
Let me check the system... Yeah, we should have it. Top shelf, aisle 7. If you don't see it, I can look in the back.
我查一下系统……有的，第7过道最上层货架。如果没看到，我去后面仓库帮你找。
Alex
That'd be great, thanks. Oh, and one more thing — is this on sale? The sign said "buy one get one" but the shelf tag says regular price.
那太好了，谢谢。哦还有件事——这个在打折吗？标牌上写着"买一送一"但货架价签上是原价。
Maria
Let me scan it for you. ... Yeah, it's on sale. The BOGO just started today, the tags just haven't been updated yet. Good eye — you just got two for the price of one.
我帮你扫一下。……在打折的。买一送一今天刚开始，价签还没来得及换。好眼力——你买到买一送一了。
Alex
Nice, I'll grab another one then. Alright, I think that's everything for me. Oh — I brought my own bags. Do I just leave them at the end of the belt?
太好了，那我再拿一罐。好了，我想我就买这些了。哦——我自己带了袋子。放传送带那头就行吗？
Maria
Exactly, just set them down at the end and I'll pack everything in there. Your total comes to $38.50.
对，放那头就行，我帮你装进去。一共三十八块五。
🎤 口语练习
1 You're at a large supermarket and can't find the pasta sauce. Ask a store employee for help using "Where would I find the...?"
你可以这样说
A："Excuse me, where would I find the pasta sauce? I've looked in the Italian section but I only see the glass jars and I want the canned kind for a recipe."
B："Hi, where would I find the soy sauce? I'm making stir-fry tonight and I've been wandering around the condiment aisle for ten minutes and I'm lost."
C："Excuse me, where would I find the laundry detergent? I always get confused — is it with the household stuff or near the cleaning supplies?"
2 You picked up an item but the shelf tag and the sale sign seem to disagree. Ask an employee "Is this on sale?"
你可以这样说
A："Excuse me, is this on sale? The sign up there says 'buy one get one half off,' but the price tag on the shelf says regular price, so I just wanted to double-check."
B："Hi, is this cereal on sale? There was a promotion sign in the last aisle but I can't tell if it applies to this size or just the family pack."
C："Quick question — is this on sale? The app says there's a discount but the shelf price looks full price to me. Could you scan it for me?"
3 You're at the checkout. The cashier asks, "Did you find everything okay?" Respond, mention you brought your own bags, and let them know that's everything.
你可以这样说
A："Yeah, I found everything, thanks. I brought my own bags — they're right there at the end. And that's everything for me, nothing else."
B："Almost — I couldn't find the oat milk but it's fine, I'll grab it next time. I brought my own bags, and that'll be all for me today."
C："Yep, got everything I need. I brought my own bags — you can just put everything in those. That's everything, ready when you are."
⚠️ 纠正提醒
❌ Where is the milk? / Where is the salt?
✅ Where would I find the milk? / What aisle is the salt in?
Where is X 没有语法错误，但语气生硬，像在命令。美国人问店员习惯用 where would I find 更委婉。超市里也可以直接问 what aisle（哪条过道），店员会直接告诉你过道号。礼貌 + 高效，一举两得。
❌ Do you sell this? / Do you have this thing?
✅ Do you guys carry...? / Do you carry...?
sell 偏书面且听起来像问"你们做零售的吗"。零售业最地道的动词是 carry = 经销/上架。you guys 是美国口语中对店员最自然的称呼——不分性别、不卑不亢。加个 guys，语气瞬间从生硬变随和。
❌ Is it cheap? / Is there discount? / How much money?
✅ Is this on sale? / Is there a sale on this?
Is it cheap 问的是"这东西廉价吗"（暗示品质差），不是问价格。问是否打折要说 is this on sale。注意 on sale 在美式英语 = 在促销/打折，不是中文"在售卖"（那是 for sale）。超市里这俩最容易搞混。
❌ Give me a bag. / I want a plastic bag.
✅ Could I get a bag? / I don't need a bag, thanks. / I brought my own bags.
give me / I want 是命令式，不礼貌。口语请求用 could I get 或 do you have。美国很多州限塑、塑料袋要收费，自带袋子很普遍——I brought my own bags 是最自然的说法，收银员一听就懂。
❌ I want to pay. / I buy these. / I finish.
✅ That's everything. / That'll be all.
在收银台说 I want to pay 很奇怪——收银员当然知道你要付钱。I buy these 和 I finish 更是中式直译。that's everything / that'll be all 是回应"还有别的吗 / 就这些？"的标准说法，带着"我买完了，可以结账了"的意思。超市结账必备金句。
