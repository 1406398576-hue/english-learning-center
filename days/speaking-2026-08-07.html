Seeing a Doctor · Describing Symptoms

在国外看医生，最怕的不是病痛本身，是进了诊室张不开嘴——症状说不清楚，问题问不出来。今天这 5 个表达，从「我最近一直感觉……」「……的时候疼」到「要不要忌口」「需要处方吗」，让你不用掏出手机翻译就能把病看明白。医生问你 What brings you in today? 的时候，别再只说 headache 一个字了。

核心表达 · 5个高频说法（每条 3 句真实美剧原句）

1. "I've been feeling..." / "I haven't been feeling great lately."
我最近一直感觉……（描述持续一段时间的症状，不是突然发生的）
使用场景医生问"what brings you in today"时，描述持续了几天的症状——头痛、乏力、恶心、没有胃口等
《Friends》S06E04 "The One Where Joey Loses His Insurance" — Joey 终于去看医生"I've been having this... this pain. Right here. And it kind of comes and goes."
《Modern Family》S02E13 "Caught in the Act" — Claire 对 Phil 抱怨身体不舒服"I haven't been feeling well all week. It's like this low-grade... something."
《The Office》S04E01 "Fun Run" — Michael 在医院试图描述症状"I've been feeling... not myself. There's a burning. And I feel like I'm... fading."
🎬 真实场景：I've been feeling 后面直接加形容词（tired / dizzy / nauseous / achy），或者 I've been having + 名词（a headache / a fever / this pain）。"一阵一阵的"用 comes and goes，"没好过"用 hasn't gone away。
2. "It hurts when I..." / "It only hurts when I..."
我……的时候会疼 / 只有……的时候才疼
使用场景医生需要定位原因——是吞咽时疼、弯腰时疼、还是不动也疼？越具体越好
《Friends》S06E04 "The One Where Joey Loses His Insurance" — Joey 描述疝气症状"It hurts when I cough. And when I laugh. Basically when I do... anything."
《The Office》S02E12 "The Injury" — Michael 描述他的脚"It hurts when I walk. It hurts when I sit. It hurts when I don't move it. I just... it hurts."
《How I Met Your Mother》S03E20 "Miracles" — Ted 在急诊室"It only hurts when I turn my neck this way. The other way is fine."
🎬 真实场景：描述疼痛有三个层次——sharp（刺痛）、dull（钝痛/隐隐作痛）、throbbing（一跳一跳地疼）。问位置用 It's on the left/right side 或 It's right here（指位置）。"不动就不疼"说 It only hurts when I...，"一直疼"说 It's constant。
3. "Is this something I should be worried about?"
这个严重吗？我需要担心吗？
使用场景描述完症状后，想知道病情严不严重——比 "Is it serious?" 更自然、更像普通人会问的话
《Friends》S06E04 — Joey 看完检查结果后紧张地问医生"So... is it bad? Like really bad? Like... not-gonna-make-it bad?"
《Modern Family》S01E03 "Come Fly with Me" — Phil 在诊所"Just tell me. Is this something I should be worried about? Because I have a condition— I can't handle bad news."
《The Office》S02E12 "The Injury" — Michael 紧张地问"Am I going to be okay? Just be straight with me. I'm a big boy."
🎬 真实场景：问严重性有三个层次——最轻的是 Should I be worried?，中等的 Is this serious?，紧张的 Am I going to be okay?。对方如果说 It's nothing serious（不严重），你就可以放心了。别直接问 Will I die?，除非你真的在演喜剧。
4. "Do I need a prescription? / Can I just get something over the counter?"
我需要处方药吗？还是买非处方药就行？
使用场景医生诊断完了，药怎么拿——需要处方去药房、还是超市就能买到
《Friends》S06E04 — Joey 在医生诊室"So do I need, like... surgery? Or can I just take something and be fine?"
《How I Met Your Mother》S02E13 "Columns" — Barney 在药房"Can I just get the over-the-counter stuff? The prescription ones make me feel like I'm 80."
《Modern Family》S04E05 "Open House of Horrors" — Phil 问药剂师"So I don't need a prescription for this? I just... take it? This feels like a loophole."
🎬 真实场景：prescription（处方药，需医生开处方去药房取）和 over-the-counter / OTC（非处方药，超市药店直接买）是两个核心词。处方上会有 dosage（剂量），问 How often should I take this? 是"一天吃几次"。
5. "Is there anything I should avoid?" / "Should I stay away from...?"
有什么注意事项/要忌口的吗？
使用场景拿完药、诊断结束后问饮食禁忌、活动限制——能不能喝酒、能不能运动、要不要休息
《Modern Family》S01E15 "My Funky Valentine" — Phil 问医生"So I should probably stay away from... spicy food? Alcohol? Fun in general?"
《Friends》S08E21 "The One with the Cooking Class" — Monica 问注意事项"Is there anything I shouldn't eat? Like... anything good?"
《The Office》S03E22 "Women's Appreciation" — Dwight 对 Michael"You should probably avoid... all physical activity. And strong emotions. And daylight."
🎬 真实场景：avoid 后面直接加名词（alcohol / spicy food / heavy lifting / dairy）。Should I stay away from... 更口语化。如果医生只是说 Get some rest and drink plenty of water，说明你这病没什么大不了的——这就是英语国家最常开的"药方"。

情景对话 · 去诊所看医生（8 轮）

🏥 场景：Emma 最近一周持续头痛、乏力、没什么胃口，终于约了家庭医生 Dr. Chen 去看诊。医生问诊、检查、给建议的完整流程。
Dr. Chen: Hi Emma, come on in. What brings you in today?
Emma 你好，请进。今天怎么不舒服？
Emma: Hi, Dr. Chen. I've been feeling really run-down for about a week now. Headaches, no energy, and I've barely been eating.
您好陈医生。我大概一个星期了，一直感觉很累，头痛，没精神，几乎吃不下东西。
Dr. Chen: Okay, let me check your vitals. When do the headaches usually hit? Is it worse in the morning or at night?
好，我先给你量一下基本体征。头痛一般什么时候发作？早上更严重还是晚上？
Emma: Usually in the afternoon. And it hurts mostly on the right side, around my temple. Like a dull, throbbing pain.
通常是下午。主要是右边疼，太阳穴附近，像那种隐隐的一跳一跳的疼。
Dr. Chen: I see. Have you been under a lot of stress lately? Sleeping okay?
明白了。最近压力大吗？睡得怎么样？
Emma: Yeah, work has been pretty intense. I've been sleeping maybe five hours a night. Is this something I should be worried about?
对，工作压力挺大的。我每晚大概只睡五小时。这个严重吗，需要担心吗？
Dr. Chen: I don't think it's anything serious — sounds like a tension headache combined with burnout. I'll prescribe something for the pain, but the real fix is rest. Is there anything else?
我觉得不严重——听上去像紧张性头痛加上过劳。我给你开点止痛药，但真正的解药是休息。还有别的问题吗？
Emma: Actually, yeah — is there anything I should avoid while taking the medication? Like coffee or alcohol?
对了，吃药期间有什么要注意的吗？比如咖啡或者酒要忌吗？
Dr. Chen: Good question. Skip the alcohol for now, and try to cut back on caffeine — it can make the headaches worse. Drink plenty of water and get at least seven hours of sleep. You should feel better in a few days.
问得好。暂时别喝酒，咖啡因也尽量少喝——它可能让头痛加重。多喝水，每天至少睡七小时。过几天应该就好了。
Emma: Got it. Thanks so much, Dr. Chen. I really appreciate it.
好的，太谢谢您了陈医生。真的很感谢。

口语练习 · 大声说出来

  1. You've had a sore throat and fever for three days. The doctor asks: "What brings you in today?" Describe your symptoms in 2-3 sentences.
    💡 用 I've been feeling... / I've had + 症状 + for + 天数 来描述。顺便告诉医生什么时候开始的（started about three days ago）。
    示例： "I've had a really bad sore throat for about three days, and I've been running a fever on and off. It hurts when I swallow, especially in the morning." "I've been feeling pretty awful — sore throat, fever, just no energy at all. It started three days ago and hasn't gotten any better." "My throat has been killing me for three days. It hurts when I swallow, and I've had a fever that comes and goes. I can barely eat anything."
  2. You hurt your ankle playing basketball over the weekend, and it still hurts when you walk. Tell the doctor what happened and ask if it's serious.
    💡 先说怎么受伤的（I twisted my ankle / I landed funny），再说什么时候疼（It hurts when I walk / put weight on it），最后问 Is this something I should be worried about?
    示例： "I twisted my ankle playing basketball on Saturday. It's not super swollen now, but it still hurts when I walk or put weight on it. Is this something I should be worried about?" "I landed funny during a game over the weekend and my ankle's been bugging me ever since. It only hurts when I walk — when I'm sitting it's fine. Should I get an X-ray?" "So I rolled my ankle pretty badly two days ago. It hurts when I try to walk normally, and it's still a little swollen. Is it possible I did more than just twist it?"
  3. The doctor prescribes you antibiotics and tells you to take them with food. Ask three follow-up questions: about side effects, what to avoid, and when you should come back.
    💡 三连问：① Are there any side effects? ② Is there anything I should avoid? ③ How long should I wait before coming back if it doesn't get better?
    示例： "Are there any side effects I should know about? Like, will it make me drowsy? And is there anything I should avoid — alcohol, certain foods? Also, if I'm not feeling better after a few days, should I come back?" "Got it — one, any side effects? Two, anything I should stay away from while I'm on this? Three, if it doesn't clear up by Friday, should I come back in or just call?" "Thanks — just a few quick questions. Should I expect any side effects? Is there anything I should avoid while taking it? And how long should I give it before coming back if I'm still not feeling better?"
🎤 建议：今天训练最重要的是三个句型——I've been feeling...（描述持续症状）、It hurts when I...（定位疼痛触发点）、Is there anything I should avoid?（问注意事项）。这三个句型放在一起，基本覆盖了普通门诊的 80% 对话。先朗读对话，直到不看中文就能顺下来，然后对着镜子大声回答 3 个练习。

纠正提醒 · 避开这些坑

❌ 不太自然 ✅ 更自然 说明
"I have headache. Yesterday start." "I've had a headache since yesterday."
"My headache started yesterday."		 描述持续症状用现在完成时 I've had... since...，不要只用一般现在时。started 和 since 搭配使用时态要对。
"My head is pain." / "I have pain in my head." "My head hurts." / "I have a headache."
"It's a sharp pain on the left side."		 pain 是名词、hurt 是动词。"疼"的日常说法是 It hurts 或 I have a headache / sore throat / stomachache，而不是 My head is pain。
"Is it big problem?" "Is it serious?"
"Is this something I should be worried about?"		 问病情严重不严重，big problem 太中式。serious 是最简洁的词，something I should be worried about 是更自然的日常说法。
"I need medicine." / "Give me some medicine." "Do I need a prescription?"
"Can I just get something over the counter?"		 跟医生说"给我药"在中文语境里可以，英文里 Give me medicine 像命令。用问句 Do I need a prescription? 既礼貌又道出了你真正的意思——要不要开药。
"I can't eat what?" / "What food I cannot eat?" "Is there anything I should avoid?"
"Are there any foods I should stay away from?"		 问忌口不要说 I cannot eat what（语序和用词都别扭）。avoid / stay away from 是地道说法，适用于食物、酒精、运动等任何注意事项。
📌 今日打卡清单：① 朗读对话 2 遍 → ② 背 5 个核心表达的 3 个关键句型（I've been feeling / It hurts when I / Is there anything I should avoid）→ ③ 用英语回答 3 个练习并录音 → ④ 回看纠正表，把 I have headache 改成 I've had a headache。

下一次身体不舒服去看医生，进诊室先深呼吸，开口就是 "Hi, I've been feeling..."，剩下的医生会一步一步问你。你只需要把症状说清楚，问题问到位。Take care and stay healthy! 🩺
