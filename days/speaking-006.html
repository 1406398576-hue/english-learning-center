今日主题

退换货与投诉

Returns, Exchanges & Complaints

买到不合适的尺码、收到有瑕疵的商品、或者店员态度让人头疼——在海外购物、网购退货、甚至和客服沟通时，这几句能让你把意思说清楚又不显生硬。今天练完，下次遇到「想退想换想投诉」都能开口。

核心表达 · 5个高频说法（每条 3 个美剧原句例句）

1. "I'd like to return this."
我想把这件退掉。
使用场景拿着商品到柜台/服务台，正式提出退货
"Excuse me, I'd like to return this. It's not what I expected at all."（买到和想象中不一样的衣服，去退货） 📺 《Friends》"The One with the Leather Pants" (S03E04)
"I'd like to return this shirt — it just doesn't look right on me."（试穿后觉得不合适想退） 📺 《The Office》"The Job" (S03E24)
"I'd like to return this, please. Do I need the receipt?"（不确定要不要小票，先问一句） 📺 《Modern Family》"Fizbo" (S02E09)
2. "Can I get a refund?"
能退款吗？/ 能把钱退给我吗？
使用场景退货时明确想拿回钱，而不是换货或店家代金券
"The thing is broken. Can I get a refund?"（东西坏了，直接问退款） 📺 《Friends》"The One with the Cop" (S05E07)
"If it doesn't fit, can I get a refund, or only store credit?"（确认是退现金还是只能换代金券） 📺 《How I Met Your Mother》"The Platinum Rule" (S04E13)
"I'd really rather get a refund than exchange it."（明确偏好退款而不是换货） 📺 《The Office》"The Return" (S03E14)
3. "I'd like to exchange it for a different size."
我想换个尺码。
使用场景商品本身没问题，只是尺码/颜色不合适，想换不退货
"I love it, but it's too small — I'd like to exchange it for a larger size."（喜欢但偏小，想换大码） 📺 《Modern Family》"The Incident" (S03E20)
"Could I exchange it for a different color? The blue one, maybe."（想换成蓝色） 📺 《Friends》"The One with the Ring" (S06E13)
"I'd like to exchange it for a different size — do you have it in medium?"（问有没有中码可换） 📺 《How I Met Your Mother》"How I Met Everyone Else" (S04E07)
4. "This is defective. / It's faulty."
这个有瑕疵。/ 它是坏的。
使用场景商品质量问题：破损、故障、做工缺陷，用来支撑退货/投诉
"Look, this is defective — the zipper's completely stuck."（拉链卡死，指商品有缺陷） 📺 《The Office》"The Merger" (S04E08)
"It arrived faulty, so I'm bringing it straight back."（收到就是坏的，直接拿回来） 📺 《Friends》"The One with the Fake Party" (S04E24)
"This is clearly defective, and I'd like a replacement, not a refund."（有瑕疵但只想换新的，不要退款） 📺 《Modern Family》"Door to Door" (S04E08)
5. "I'd like to speak to the manager."
我想找经理谈谈。
使用场景店员解决不了、或态度/政策让你不满意时，要求升级处理
"This isn't acceptable. I'd like to speak to the manager, please."（问题没解决，礼貌但坚定地要找经理） 📺 《The Office》"The Client" (S02E05)
"I'm not leaving until I speak to the manager about this."（坚持要见经理） 📺 《How I Met Your Mother》"The Naked Man" (S04E09)
"Could I speak to your manager? There's been a misunderstanding."（语气更缓和，说是有误会要澄清） 📺 《Friends》"The One with the Kips" (S05E05)

情景对话 · 7轮真实英语

🛍️ 场景：Emma 在服装店买了一件外套，回家发现内衬开线、尺码也偏紧，于是拿回店里要求退换。
Emma: Hi, I'd like to return this coat. The lining is coming apart, and it's a bit too tight.
你好，我想退掉这件外套。内衬开线了，而且有点偏紧。
Clerk: I'm sorry about that. Do you have the receipt with you?
很抱歉。您带小票了吗？
Emma: Yes, here it is. Honestly, this is defective — I only wore it once.
带了，在这儿。说实话这衣服有瑕疵，我就穿了一次。
Clerk: I see. Would you prefer a refund, or would you like to exchange it for a different size?
我明白了。您是想要退款，还是想换个尺码？
Emma: I'd like to exchange it for a medium, if you have one in this color.
如果同色有中码的话，我想换个中码。
Clerk: Let me check in the back... Unfortunately, we're out of medium. Can I offer you a store credit instead?
我到后面查一下……抱歉，中码卖完了。可以给您换成店家代金券吗？
Emma: Hmm, I'd really rather get a refund. If that's a problem, I'd like to speak to the manager.
嗯，我更想要退款。如果这有问题的话，我想找经理谈谈。

口语练习 · 大声说出来

  1. Describe a time you returned something to a store. What was wrong with it, and how did the clerk react?
    💡 用上 "I'd like to return this" + "This is defective"，讲清问题和对方反应。
    示例： "Last month I returned a pair of shoes because the sole was coming off. The clerk was really nice and gave me a full refund." "I once took back a blender that was faulty. They didn't have a replacement, so I asked for a refund." "I returned a shirt that was too small and exchanged it for a larger size instead."
  2. You bought a gift that doesn't fit the person. How do you ask to exchange it without the receipt?
    💡 试试 "I'd like to exchange it" + "I don't have the receipt, but..." 的委婉说法。
    示例： "Hi, I'd like to exchange this for a different size. I don't have the receipt, but it still has the tag on it." "I bought this as a gift and it doesn't fit. Could I exchange it without a receipt?" "The size is wrong — would you accept an exchange if I show you the tag?"
  3. A shop refuses to refund a broken item. What do you say to stay calm but firm, and ask for the manager?
    💡 用 "This is clearly defective" + "I'd like to speak to the manager, please."
    示例： "This is clearly defective, and I'd like a refund. If you can't help, I'd like to speak to the manager, please." "I'm not angry, but the policy says I can return a faulty item. Could I talk to your manager?" "It broke the first time I used it. I'd really like to speak to someone who can sort this out."
🎤 建议：先把对话朗读 2-3 遍，注意 "refund / exchange / defective / receipt" 的发音；然后回答 3 个练习问题并录音回听，重点检查 "exchange" 和 "receipt" 有没有读准。

纠正提醒 · 避开这些坑

❌ 不太自然 ✅ 更自然 说明
"I want to return back this." "I'd like to return this." return 本身已经含「退回来」的意思，后面加 back 是典型中式冗余。礼貌用 "I'd like to..." 比 "I want to..." 更得体。
"Give me money back." "Can I get a refund?" 口语里「退款」最地道是 refund 这个名词/动词，店员也最听得懂。
"I want to change it." "I'd like to exchange it." change 在购物场景容易误会成「换零钱 (change)」或「改变主意」。换货固定用 exchange。
"It doesn't match me." "It doesn't fit."
"It's the wrong size."		 说尺码不合身用 fit；match 通常指颜色/风格「搭不搭」，不是「合不合身」。
"Speak to your manager!" "Could I speak to the manager, please?" 投诉时也要保持礼貌，用 "Could I... please?" 既坚定又不显攻击性，更容易解决问题。
"The quality is not good." "This is defective."
"It's faulty."		 说商品「有质量问题/坏了」用 defective / faulty 更精准，也更有说服力支撑退货。
📌 今日打卡清单：① 朗读对话2遍 → ② 背5个核心表达（return / refund / exchange / defective / manager）→ ③ 回答3个练习题（录音）→ ④ 回顾纠正表，别再说 "return back" 和 "change it"。
