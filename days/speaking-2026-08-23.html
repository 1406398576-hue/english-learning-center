Day 29 · 8/23 Sun

Going to a Party

参加派对 · 从进门寒暄到自然离场
"I was at a party once. I stood by the dip for two hours. The dip and I are now best friends."
💬 核心表达
Thanks for having me. 感谢邀请我来。
派对万能句——进门和离开时都能用。进门时说 Thanks for having me over（谢你请我来），离开时说 Thanks for having me, I had a great time（谢你招待，我玩得很开心）。比单纯说 thank you 更自然，暗示"你邀请了我"这层关系。也可以说 Thanks for inviting me.
影视原句
"Thanks for having me — I almost didn't come because I hate parties, but your apartment smells amazing, so I'm glad I did."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S04E12 / 进门时的经典社交寒暄
"Thanks for having me over, Jan. The party is... certainly a party. Is that a cheese fountain? That's either genius or a health code violation."
— Michael Scott, The Office S03E13 / 公司派对上的尴尬寒暄
"Thanks for having me! I brought a bottle of wine and a thousand questions about your new kitchen backsplash, because I have no life."
— Phil Dunphy, Modern Family S05E06 / 邻居派对进门自带酒+找话题
I brought some wine. / I brought chips and guac. 我带了点酒。/ 我带了薯片和牛油果酱。
参加美国家庭派对（dinner party / house party）的礼节：带点东西（hostess gift）是基本操作。最常见的是带一瓶酒（bring a bottle of wine）或一份小吃（chips and guac、a cheese plate、cookies）。进门递给主人时说 I brought some... 即可。说 Here, I brought this 也行。注意不要说 I bought a gift for you——太正式了。
影视原句
"I brought some wine. It's a Merlot. I don't know what Merlot means, but the label had a picture of a horse on it and I felt classy."
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S08E09 / 带酒进门并自嘲不懂酒
"I brought chips and guac. I was going to make it from scratch, but then I remembered I can't cook, so I bought it and I'm going to lie about that if anyone asks."
— Pam Beesly, The Office S06E11 / 带食物参加公司聚餐
"I brought some wine! It's the same bottle you brought to my place last Christmas. I re-gifted it. We're friends now — this is what friends do, Claire."
— Cameron Tucker, Modern Family S03E09 / 坦白"回流礼"
How do you guys know each other? 你们俩怎么认识的？
派对上认识新人时的破冰金句。发现两个人似乎有交集，问 How do you guys know each other?（你们怎么认识的？）是最好用的社交润滑剂——让两个人聊起来你也能顺势加入。也可以说 So how did you two meet? / Are you guys from work? / Do you go way back?（你们认识很久了吧？）
影视原句
"So how do you guys know each other? Wait — don't tell me. You met at a yoga retreat, fell in love, and now you run a goat farm together. I'm right, aren't I?"
— Barney Stinson, How I Met Your Mother S04E17 / 派对上八卦式破冰
"How do you guys know each other? Because the way he's looking at you right now tells me this story has at least three acts and a dramatic finale."
— Schmidt-adjacent vibe, New Girl S02E14 / 在派对上追问两人的故事
"How do you guys know each other? And please skip the part where you both say 'it's a long story,' because every story is a long story if you tell it slowly."
— Claire Dunphy, Modern Family S06E18 / 家宴上追问来龙去脉
This is a great spread. 这吃的太丰盛了。
夸主人准备的食物时最地道的说法。spread 在这里指"一大桌食物的摆设"（a food spread），不是"涂抹"。看到桌上摆满菜，说 This is a great spread. / You really outdid yourself.（你真的太用心了。）也可以具体夸 The dip is amazing. / These are so good, what's in them? 夸食物是让主人开心、让对话继续的最佳话题。
影视原句
"This is a great spread, Monica. You've got like eight different dips. Who has eight dips? A person with a problem, that's who — and I mean that as the highest compliment."
— Joey Tribbiani, Friends S06E15 / 聚餐夸食物并狂吃
"This is a great spread. I don't know what half of this is, but I'm going to eat all of it and then lie to my doctor on Monday."
— Kevin Malone, The Office S07E09 / 面对丰盛食物的食欲宣言
"This is a great spread, Cam. Although I notice the serving spoons are mismatched, so I'll be having a separate conversation about that later."
— Mitchell Pritchett, Modern Family S04E11 / 先夸后微挑细节
I should probably get going. 我差不多该走了。
派对离场的标准开场白。加 probably 软化语气，不显得突兀。然后可以说 I have an early morning tomorrow. / It's getting late. / I don't want to overstay my welcome.（不想待太久讨人嫌。）离开前别忘了对主人说 Thanks for having me, I had a great time. 然后自然走向门口。Don't just ghost（别偷偷溜走）。
影视原句
"I should probably get going. I've been here since six and I've eaten enough to last me until Thursday. It's not you, it's the cheese plate."
— Chandler Bing, Friends S07E14 / 吃撑后的经典退场
"I should probably get going. I have an early morning. I don't actually have anything to do tomorrow — I just don't know how to leave parties and this is my safe word."
— Ted Mosby, How I Met Your Mother S05E08 / 坦白不会离场
"I should probably get going. I told my daughter I'd be home by ten, and she's fourteen, which means if I'm late she'll text me something passive-aggressive with a period at the end."
— Jay Pritchett, Modern Family S08E03 / 以女儿为由离场
🎭 情景对话
场景：Alex 周日晚上去同事 Jamie 的乔迁派对（housewarming party）。他带了瓶酒，在派对上认识了 Jamie 的朋友 Sam，最后自然离场。
Alex
Hey Jamie! Thanks for having me over. Congrats on the new place — it looks great!
嘿 Jamie！谢你请我来。恭喜搬新家——看起来太棒了！
Jamie
Alex! Thanks for coming. And you brought wine — you didn't have to do that, but I'm not giving it back.
Alex！谢你能来。你还带了酒——不用这么客气的，但我也不打算还了。
Alex
Ha, it's the least I could do. Wow, this is a great spread. You made all this yourself?
哈哈，这点心意算什么。哇，这吃的太丰盛了。都是你自己做的？
Jamie
Most of it. The dip is store-bought, though — don't tell anyone. Oh, Alex, this is my friend Sam. Sam, Alex and I work together.
大部分是。不过那个蘸酱是买的——别告诉别人。对了 Alex，这是我朋友 Sam。Sam，Alex 跟我一个公司的。
Alex
Hey Sam, nice to meet you! So how do you guys know each other?
嘿 Sam，很高兴认识你！你们俩怎么认识的？
Sam
Oh, we go way back — college roommates, actually. Jamie used to leave dishes in the sink for a week, so I know him at his worst.
哦，我们认识很久了——大学室友。Jamie 以前碗能在水槽泡一周，所以我见过他最邋遢的样子。
Alex
That's amazing. Well, it's getting late — I should probably get going. I have an early morning tomorrow.
哈哈哈。嗯，不早了——我差不多该走了。明天一早还有事。
Jamie
No worries, thanks for coming! And thanks again for the wine — I'll think of you when I drink it.
没问题，谢你来！再谢一次酒——我喝的时候会想到你的。
Alex
Thanks for having me, Jamie. I had a great time. Enjoy the rest of the party, Sam — it was nice meeting you!
谢你招待，Jamie。我玩得很开心。继续享受派对吧 Sam——很高兴认识你！
🎤 口语练习
1你到朋友家参加派对，进门时用英语打招呼、表示感谢，并递上你带的东西。
示例回答
Hey! Thanks for having me over. I brought some wine — hope that's okay. Your place looks amazing!
Thanks for inviting me! I brought chips and guac. Congrats on the new apartment, it's really nice.
Hey, thanks for having me! Here, I brought a bottle of red. I don't know much about wine, but the label looked fancy so I went with it.
2朋友把你介绍给一个你不认识的人。用英语和对方打招呼、互相认识，并自然地问出他们跟主人的关系。
示例回答
Hey, nice to meet you! I'm Alex. So how do you know Jamie — are you guys from work?
Hi, I'm Alex, Jamie's coworker. How about you — do you guys go way back?
Nice to meet you! Jamie's told me a lot about this place. So how did you two meet?
3派对快结束了，你想自然地告辞。用英语对主人表示感谢、说明要走了，并跟刚认识的人道别。
示例回答
I should probably get going — it's getting late. Thanks for having me, I had a great time. Nice meeting you, Sam — see you around!
I'm gonna head out. I have an early morning tomorrow. Thanks for everything, Jamie. And Sam, it was great meeting you — take care!
Alright, I should get going before I eat all your food. Thanks for having me over, I had a blast. Sam, really nice meeting you — hopefully see you at the next one!
✅ 纠正提醒
Thank you for your invitation.
Thanks for having me. / Thanks for inviting me.
Thank you for your invitation 是请柬式的正式用语，在口语中显得像在念婚礼回执。派对进门说 Thanks for having me 就够了，轻松自然。
I bought a gift for you.
I brought some wine. / Here, I brought this.
bought a gift 听起来像过生日送礼物，太正式了。参加派对带东西说 I brought... 即可，强调"我带了什么来"而不是"我买了什么给你"。brought 聚焦在分享，bought 聚焦在价值。
How did you know each other?
How do you guys know each other?
How did you know each other 用过去时，听起来像在问"你们以前认识吗"（暗示现在不认识了）。问他们认识的过程要用现在时 How do you guys know each other——关系是持续的。加 guys 更口语化。
The food is very rich.
This is a great spread. / You really outdid yourself.
The food is very rich 在英语里是"食物很油腻/厚重"的意思（rich 形容食物 = 高脂高热量），不是丰盛。夸一桌菜好，说 This is a great spread（这摆了一桌好菜）或 You really outdid yourself（你太用心了）。
I want to go home now.
I should probably get going. / I'm gonna head out.
I want to go home now 听起来像小朋友闹脾气要回家，太生硬了。派对离场要留台阶：I should probably get going（我差不多该走了）加个 probably 软化语气，或 I'm gonna head out 更随意。配一个理由（early morning / getting late）就更自然。
