今日主题

和朋友约计划

Making Plans with Friends

约饭、约周末、临时起意做点什么——这大概是日常最高频的对话场景之一了。今天这5个表达+1段对话，练完就能直接用。

核心表达 · 5个高频说法（每条 3 个美国人真实日常例句）

1. "Are you free this weekend?"
你这周末有空吗？
使用场景想约人 / 问对方这周末有空没
"Hey, are you free this weekend? Wanna grab coffee or something?"（约朋友喝咖啡）
"Are you free Saturday afternoon? I was thinking we could catch a movie."（约下午看电影）
"You free this weekend at all? We're having a small BBQ at my place."（邀对方来家里烧烤）
2. "I'm down for that."
我可以啊 / 没问题，算我一个
使用场景同意对方提议 / 答应一起行动
"A road trip this summer? Yeah, I'm totally down for that."（爽快答应去公路旅行）
"Pizza for dinner? I'm down."（随口答应吃披萨）
"You wanna hit the gym after work? I'm down for that."（答应下班去健身）
3. "Let me check and get back to you."
我确认一下再回复你
使用场景不确定有没有空，不想当场答应，先拖一下
"Let me check my schedule and get back to you, okay?"（先查日程再回）
"I'll look at my calendar and get back to you in a bit."（看下日历稍后回复）
"Let me check with my wife and I'll let you know."（先跟家人确认再给答复）
4. "Works for me."
可以 / 这个时间对我来说合适
使用场景对方提议时间/地点，你觉得合适
"Seven o'clock? Yeah, that works for me."（确认七点可以）
"How about the place near the station? Works for me."（确认地点合适）
"Friday's good. That works."（确认周五没问题）
5. "It's been a while!"
好久不见啊！
使用场景和一阵子没见的朋友重逢时开场
"Hey! It's been a while — how've you been?"（重逢寒暄）
"It's been a while since we last talked. What's new?"（好久没聊，问近况）
"Oh wow, it's been a while! Long time no see."（惊讶好久不见）

情景对话 · 8轮真实聊天

📱 场景：Sarah 和 Mike 在微信上聊天，约周六一起吃饭。
Sarah: Hey Mike! It's been a while. How've you been?
嘿 Mike！好久不见，最近怎么样？
Mike: Hey Sarah! I've been good, just busy with work. You?
嘿 Sarah！挺好的，就是工作忙。你呢？
Sarah: Same here. Hey, are you free this Saturday? I was thinking we could grab lunch somewhere.
我也是。嘿，你周六有空吗？我想着咱俩找个地方吃个午饭。
Mike: Saturday... let me check. Yeah, I should be free. What did you have in mind?
周六……我看看。应该有空。你想去哪儿？
Sarah: There's this new ramen place downtown. I've been wanting to try it.
市中心新开了一家拉面店，我一直想去试试。
Mike: Oh, I'm down for that. I love ramen. What time?
哦，我可以啊。我爱吃拉面。几点？
Sarah: How about 1 PM? That way we don't have to rush in the morning.
下午一点怎么样？这样早上不用赶。
Mike: Works for me. Want me to book a table?
可以。要我订个位吗？
Sarah: That'd be great. Just text me the details. Can't wait!
太好了。把具体信息发我就行。期待！
Mike: Will do. See you Saturday!
没问题。周六见！

口语练习 · 大声说出来

  1. What do you usually do on weekends? Describe one thing you did last weekend.
    💡 用到现在时（usually）+ 过去时（did），注意时态切换。
    示例： "I usually just chill at home, but last weekend I went hiking with some friends. It was awesome." "On weekends I normally catch up on sleep. This Saturday I tried that new café downtown—the coffee was great." "I usually hang out with my roommates. Last Sunday we cooked dinner together and watched a movie."
  2. A friend asks you to hang out this Friday, but you're busy. How do you say no politely and suggest another time?
    💡 试试用 "I'd love to, but..." 开头，再用 "How about..." 改约时间。
    示例： "I'd love to, but I'm slammed with work on Friday. How about Saturday afternoon instead?" "That sounds fun, but I've already got plans. Can we do Sunday? I'm free then." "I'm super busy this Friday, sorry! Maybe next week? Let me check and get back to you."
  3. Think of a restaurant you'd like to try. Invite a friend to go with you — in English.
    💡 用上今天学的 "I've been wanting to try..." 和 "Are you free...?"
    示例： "Hey, are you free this weekend? I've been wanting to try this new ramen place. Wanna go together?" "There's a hotpot spot everyone's been talking about. I'm down to check it out—you in?" "Are you free Saturday for lunch? I've been wanting to try that Mexican place. Count me in?"
🎤 建议：把对话大声朗读 2-3 遍，感受节奏。然后回答问题并录音回听，你会发现自己哪里卡壳。

纠正提醒 · 避开这些坑

❌ 不太自然 ✅ 更自然 说明
"Are you convenient?" "Does it work for you?"
"Is that a good time?"		 convenient 形容的是事物不是人。问别人方不方便，别说 "are you convenient"。
"I have time." "I'm free."
"I'm available."		 "I have time" 语法没错，但口语里老外更常说 "I'm free"。
"Let's eat together." "Let's grab lunch."
"Wanna grab a bite?"		 "eat together" 太书面，日常用 "grab food / grab a bite / grab lunch"。
"What time do you have?" "What time works for you?" 问对方什么时候方便，"works for you" 是最自然的说法。
"I will go with you." "I'm down."
"Count me in."
"I'm in."		 答应一起行动，这些比 "I will go with you" 地道太多。
